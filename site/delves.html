Atal'Aman

The short version

  • West Zul'Aman (Eversong border).
  • Ritual Interrupted, Toadly Unbecoming, Totem Annihilation.
  • Final boss on all: Spiritflayer Jin'Ma.

Route

  • Ritual: rescue kidnapped Spiritpaw furbolgs across the delve.
  • Toadly: dispel hexed Amani with Vilebranch Hex Stick while killing invaders.
  • Totem: destroy cursed totems to free Akil'zon; use eagles for alternate approaches.

Trash

  • Interrupt Vilebranch casts.
  • Totem variant: prioritize totems on the path.
  • Pull beasts away from hexed NPCs you are trying to save.

Bosses

  • flaying knife splits spirits — each collected spirit gives +10% damage (pick them up deliberately).
  • Dodge raging spirits on the floor.
  • Before claim spirits ends, grab your spirits or Jin'Ma gains their buff.

Collegiate Calamity

The short version

  • Northwest Silvermoon / Eversong Woods (Thalassian University).
  • Academy Under Siege -> Voidscorned Vagrant.
  • Faculty of Fear -> Infiltrator Garand.
  • Invasive Glow -> Hydrangea.
  • An Elementary Antidote -> no final boss; treasure spawns instead of a kill.

Route

  • Under Siege: close void portals and kill Devouring Host.
  • Faculty of Fear: Eye of Revelation — suspicious students glow yellow through walls.
  • Invasive Glow: Deweeder kills small Lightbloom and damages large ones.
  • Clear Luminibulb patches on the main level.
  • An Elementary Antidote: speak to Sir Finley Mrrgglton, gather 10 Research Tomes, then collect ingredients and cure 7 Envenomed Denizens. Ingredients are worth different amounts, so grab the big ones.
  • Fungal Pharmacon is a weapon too: it clears the poison pools AND hits everything within 5 yards of where it lands.

Trash

  • Under Siege: prioritize portal closers.
  • Faculty: reveal disguised students, then kill before they ambush.
  • Glow: channel Deweeder while moving.
  • Do not skip Luminibulb patches or the run drags.

Bosses

  • Hydrangea (Glow): kill wildroot weave roots before lightbloom salvo; dodge light zones.
  • Garand (Faculty): dodge shadow laceration or use a defensive; spread for twilight crash jumps.
  • Voidscorned Vagrant (Siege): sidestep void eruption; always interrupt terrifying power.

Gnarldor Isle

The short version

  • New in 12.1, on the Coiled Isle — entrance at Gnarldor Isle (64.3, 77.7). Scrollmaster Ruma at the entrance starts a short quest chain.
  • Three story variants, two bosses (per Method and Icy Veins — not yet measured on your client). Two variants end at Gralka Snake-Eater: she eats snakes for damage stacks and leaves venom puddles — drag her off them, sidestep the waves of her Purging Breath.
  • The third ends at Osseous Amalgamation: interrupt his bone shield, run from Bonestorm, dodge the bone spikes.
  • Click the Tortollan Scrolls you pass — buffs, some with a catch.

Route

  • Three Sturdy Chests — click to set a waypoint: Sturdy Chest 1 (60.44, 68.12) · Sturdy Chest 2 (52.41, 40.84) · Sturdy Chest 3 (28.67, 41.69).
  • You arrive at about 77, 46; the exit portal stands right there — sweep the chests and you end where you began.

Parhelion Plaza

The short version

  • West Isle of Quel'Danas (Sunwell area).
  • Unlocks with March on Quel'Danas (late March 2026).
  • Three variants; tough boss on all: Gladius Slaurna.
  • One of the harder Midnight delves.

Route

  • Bombing Run: Improvised Arcane Device for void portals (40s carry limit; drop in combat).
  • Holding the Line: Tempest Keep weapons + rebuild barricades.
  • March of the Arcane Parade: Sentinel machines destroy void pylons.

Trash

  • Interrupt Sacrificial Voidcaller Void Bolt whenever possible.
  • Bombing Run: do not lose portals by stepping out at the wrong time.
  • Clear Devouring Host waves before Slaurna.

Bosses

  • Kill three Sacrificial Voidcallers quickly (interrupt Void Bolt).
  • If any live through devouring nova, Slaurna gains damage done and damage reduction — wipe risk on high tiers.
  • Dodge voidscar raze (ground line).
  • Fight near center to avoid knockback off the platform.

Shadowguard Point

The short version

  • West Voidstorm (north of Abundant Voidburrow).
  • Three variants vs Shadowguard Ethereals.
  • Final boss on all: Chief-Arcanist Patram.

Route

  • Calamitous: overload Void Stabilizers with Arcane Charges (speed buff; explosions kill mobs — do not stand in them).
  • Captured Wildlife: free void creatures (their AoE can still hit you).
  • Stolen Mana: pick up the rifle and use Galvanic Blast on the mana containers until the Arcane Barrier drops.

Trash

  • Calamitous: plan overloads safely while carrying Arcane Charges.
  • Ethereal packs: interrupt casts.
  • Wildlife variant: give freed creatures space.

Bosses

  • Interrupt void bolt patram.
  • Kill Void Emissary before submit to the void ends (you get Vers + CDR; Patram gets the buff if you fail).
  • Sidestep discordant hymn void zones (slow + damage).

Sunkiller Sanctum

The short version

  • East Voidstorm (Voidspire entrance).
  • Three variants; Esuritus is final boss on two.
  • Not What I Expected: no boss — ends after 3 Corrupted Umbraroot kills.

Route

  • Core of the Problem: portals + stop Domanaar stealing Energized Orbs (orb buff: speed + damage reduction).
  • Not What I Expected: kill Lightbloom and Domanaar units, then 3 Corrupted Umbraroot.
  • Gravitational Effect: use micro-singularities to navigate and disable boss shields.

Trash

  • Kill Voidcallers quickly on every pack — they empower Esuritus later.
  • Interrupt Arcane Deluge where it appears.
  • Pick up orbs in Core variant for survivability.

Bosses

  • Kill all Voidcallers before gorge or he gains +damage for 30s per devour.
  • Interrupt calling bolt.
  • Dispel coalescing malediction or kill the add it spawns.
  • Dodge crushing rift (spawns 4 Voidcallers).

The Darkway

The short version

  • North Silvermoon / Eversong Woods (the Arcway).
  • Focusers Under Pressure, Leyline Technician, Ogre Powered (7 Unstable Aberrations).
  • Final boss on all runs: Infiltrator Gulkat.

Route

  • Focusers: find ley line focusers and return them to the delve center.
  • Leyline: tap conduits once per line to empower three focus crystals.
  • Ogre Powered: kill 7 Unstable Aberrations in the middle section before Gulkat.

Trash

  • Interrupt Twilight Seekers and Arcane Deluge on aberrations.
  • Leyline variant: kill Twilight's Blade while routing power.
  • Pull away from corners in narrow Arcway halls.

Bosses

  • Interrupt Twilight Seekers.
  • Dodge abyssal burst (frontal cone).
  • illusory deceit: exploding illusions — keep distance while handling Gulkat.

The Grudge Pit

The short version

  • Southeast Harandar.
  • Lightbloom Invasion -> Brightthorn.
  • Arena Champion -> Gyrospore.
  • Dastardly Rotstalk -> Mycomight.

Route

  • Arena Champion: wave arena fights (Sporbits help).
  • Dastardly Rotstalk: heel role — punt fans, taunt crowd (can move while taunting).
  • Lightbloom Invasion: waves, free fighters to blow up spawn points, build defenses.

Trash

  • Arena: use Sporbits for damage.
  • Invasion: clear waves before boss.
  • Rotstalk: keep crowd control — fans hurt if ignored.

Bosses

  • Brightthorn: sidestep solar charge (leaves Overbloom zones); interrupt bloom thorn; turn away before blinding burst.
  • Gyrospore: dodge fungalstorm zones, then burst during 10s dizzy; step back from fungsplosion; sidestep fungal charge.
  • Mycomight: drop rancid rain puddles away from fight area; dodge fungis fist shockwaves; sidestep fling chair.

The Gulf of Memory

The short version

  • West Harandar (Den of Echoes / Har'alnor).
  • Three variants.
  • Bosses: Lumenia (Munchies / Sporasaur) and Mul'tha'ul (Descent of the Haranir).

Route

  • Alnmoth Munchies: extra-action Alnmoth Storm while moving — devour bushes or help vs Rutaani.
  • Descent: light giant candles; more damage taken in dark areas.
  • Sporasaur: kick green-reticle Sporbits into Sporasaurs to remove their shield.

Trash

  • Stay in lit areas during Descent.
  • Kick Sporbits before they explode.
  • Channel Alnmoth Storm while moving — you can move and cast.

Bosses

  • Lumenia: kill radiant command add before it reaches you (explodes).
  • Dodge searing light patches unless using them on the add.
  • Mul'tha'ul: interrupt or dispel hopeless curse.
  • Kite away from tear it down tentacles.
  • Keep distance during unanswered call (high damage, slow walk).

The Ring of Glory

The short version

  • New in 12.1, on the Coiled Isle — entrance at The Ring of Glory (71.1, 56.4). It is an arena and it means it: you face a series of opponents rather than one boss at the end.
  • What that series is changes between runs. Sometimes it is a single boss after clearing the ground; on one Tier 11 run it was seven encounters — the ground first (nemeses, no ordinary trash), then Crushfoot, the Bluegill Brothers (Lurgle and Smurgle, with adds), Brinebeater, Guth'kar the Bound, three Arena champions at once, and Drakta last. So this is an example, not a checklist: meeting a name that is not here means the arena rolled differently, not that you did something wrong.
  • Crushfoot charges, and the charge is what kills people. Crowd control stops it — a stun or a knockback, confirmed in game. Players also report two towers with glowing blue orbs that teleport you across when he starts; we have not tested those ourselves.
  • Drakta drops a Soul Cleave circle that leaves a zone behind — stay out of it and drag him clear. He also pulls you to him. There are pillars, but there was no time to react to the pull, so treat it as damage to survive rather than something to dodge.
  • The animal rescue ends at Gnok, and he rises again undead halfway — so you kill him twice.
  • Floor traps everywhere — watch your step.
  • Interrupt the Spiritcallers’ self-heal and kill the War Drum the Warsingers plant.

Route

  • Three Sturdy Chests — click to set a waypoint: Sturdy Chest 1 (44.16, 22.60) · Sturdy Chest 2 (25.19, 73.74) · Sturdy Chest 3 (48.56, 94.84).
  • The exit is at Exit (80.10, 53.69).

Watch out

  • The golem's Fissuring Slam cannot be interrupted. A kick does nothing — the cast bar carries the shield.
  • It lands underneath you, so there is nothing to walk out of. Stun it, or use a defensive and take the hit.
  • It only shows up in the higher tiers, and not alone: on Tier 11 there were three of them, roaming loose. They can be pulled one at a time — do that. Two together is two slams you cannot interrupt.
  • Midnight Helper cannot warn you the moment it starts: since 12.1 an addon can no longer see which spell an enemy is casting. That is why this note is here instead.

The Shadow Enclave

The short version

  • Southwest Eversong Woods (Ruins of Deatholme).
  • Three variants: Mirror Shine, Shadowy Supplies, Traitor's Due.
  • Traitor's Due: stop rituals, void orbs, cultists (leveling).
  • Final boss every run: Lord Antenorian.

Route

  • Mirror Shine: carry mirrors through dark halls; hit Shadow Nexuses to spread light (avoids grues).
  • Shadowy Supplies: loot Twilight's Blade valuables from mobs or the ground.
  • Traitor's Due: chase Antenorian through the delve to the boss room.

Trash

  • In mirror light, you and enemies are Dazzled (higher crit chance).
  • Interrupt shadow casters.
  • Clear Twilight's Blade during Supplies.
  • Do not stack in hazardous ground; pull carefully in tight corridors.

Bosses

  • Interrupt shadow bolt whenever you can.
  • Teleports leave a damaging puddle — keep him near the center.
  • shadowveil annihilation: lethal channel — destroy 3 Shadow Orbs (only the unshielded orb takes damage; each kill unshields the next).
  • All orbs dead breaks his shield, stops the channel, and he takes extra damage briefly.
  • Save burst for orbs; Valeera helps break them.

Torment's Rise

The short version

  • Voidstorm — Season 1 Nemesis Delve (capstone).
  • Tier ? after any T7 with 1+ life; Tier ?? after any T10 with 1+ life.
  • Boss: Nullaeus.
  • Healer Valeera strongly recommended on Tier ??.

Route

  • Dedicated instance portal (not a rotating world delve).
  • Use Adventure Guide (Shift-J) or TomTom.
  • Weekly bounty: Beacon of Hope can pull Nullaeus into a normal delve at 50% instead.

Trash

  • Pactsworn mobs live in regular delves, not inside Torment's Rise.
  • Pace cooldowns — save defensives and interrupts for Nullaeus.

Bosses

  • Interrupt devouring essence every cast (DoT + feeds umbral rage stacks).
  • dread portal: 100% damage reduction until all adds die — full AoE burst on spawn.
  • oblivion shell phase is a DPS check.
  • umbral rage: +10% damage per stack if adds or DoTs linger — kill adds fast.

Twilight Crypts

The short version

  • Southwest Zul'Aman (Broken Throne).
  • Loosed Loa (avoid Mot'amra), Party Crasher, Trapped!
  • Final boss on all: Blademaster Darza.

Route

  • Loosed Loa: Evasive Elixir to explore; kill Skeleton Charmers and totems — track Mot'amra, do not cross his path.
  • Party Crasher: escort Explorer, avoid bear traps, open levels with levers.
  • Trapped: save hostages and find levers through the maze.

Trash

  • Mot'amra hits hard with knockback if you walk into him.
  • Party Crasher: watch trap tiles.
  • Interrupt Twilight's Blade summoners.

Bosses

  • Sidestep shade cleave — fighting close prevents dark pursuit gap-closer.
  • bask in the twilight leaves a void zone.
  • Drag Darza out of it or she gains +30% damage while standing inside.

Venomfall Deeps

The short version

  • The Season 2 Nemesis delve, in the middle of the northern island on the Coiled Isle. Azta'rec is the boss.
  • Bring the recommended item level or the fight is not close: 290 for ? and 309 for ??. Those two numbers come from the delve entrance itself, so they are the game's own advice.
  • Do a normal delve first if you are short. A Tier 8 Bountiful Coffer is 295, and its heavy chest can leave you with Blessing of Potency — +10% secondary stats for a full day, until your next delve. Coming in with that is a different fight.
  • Valeera goes on Healer for almost everyone: she removes Void Toxin, which most specs cannot. Set her to DPS only if you heal yourself, and never to Tank here.
  • Sources: the ability names and the intermission rules below come from a community guide, not from measurement. Treat them as a good map, not as gospel.

Watch out

  • Wrath of Ula'tek kills you from full health. Nature damage, and your own death recap marks it Avoidable — so there is a way out and no defensive to press.
  • It fires during the intermission, when the room is split into four quarters and three of them flood. Being caught also leaves you with +500% Nature damage taken for the rest of the fight, so a single miss ends the attempt even if you survive it.
  • This delve gives out no coordinates. Blizzard blocked position tracking in here, so the route arrow and every waypoint stay silent — that is not Midnight Helper failing.

Bosses

  • His abilities repeat in a fixed order: Noxious Bile, Void Toxin, Soul Extinction, Venom Storm. So you always know the third one is the kick.
  • Soul Extinction is the one-shot. Interrupt it every time. Save your kick for it and let Valeera dispel Void Toxin instead of spending the interrupt there.
  • Noxious Bile is a frontal that leaves pools. Aim it at the edge of the room and step out, so the middle stays clean for the intermission.
  • Venom Storm sends slow waves from the pools at the rim. Fighting near the entrance keeps them away from your back.
  • At 90%, 60% and 30% he channels Sermon of Ula'tek and takes almost no damage — stop attacking and watch. He shows the safe quarter each step, then replays the whole sequence with nothing shown. Remember the order, not just the first one.
  • Move to the middle before each of those thresholds; he is fast and you can be caught outside. Four ground markers turn the pattern into numbers — type /mh mark before you pull.
