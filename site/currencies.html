Restored Coffer Key

  • Opens Midnight delves (like previous expansion keys).
  • Earn from world content, weeklies, and vendors; no hard weekly cap on holding keys.
  • Shown on Delves & Vault with your current count.

Coffer Key Shards

  • Combine into Restored Coffer Keys (100 shards -> 1 key at the standard rate).
  • Weekly earn cap applies to shards — track \"Weekly: X / Y\" on the Delves tab.
  • Cap fills faster if you run more delves or complete shard sources.

Undercoin

  • Vendor currency for delve-related goods (curios, upgrades, convenience items).
  • Earn primarily from running delves and delve rewards.
  • Spend before hoarding blindly — check the delve vendor when you have a goal piece.

Untainted Mana-Crystals

  • Used for specific Midnight gear upgrades / vendor purchases (check current patch notes for exact vendors).
  • Earn from delves, world content, and weekly sources.
  • Account snapshot can show per-character totals.

Field Accolades

  • Shared currency for the Void & Rituals systems (Ritual Sites + Void Assaults).
  • Weekly earn cap — after cap you still play for other rewards but stop gaining accolades until reset.
  • Spends on renown rewards at the Bazaar hub (Eversong / Zul'Aman).
  • Live count on Void & Rituals tab.

Crests

  • The currency you spend to upgrade gear one rank at a time — not a crafting material, and not tied to your Great Vault raid slots.
  • Live crest counts are on Basics -> Crests (not in this list).
  • The cap is on how many you can hold, not how many you can earn in a week.
  • Not the same as delve keys — see that guide for what to spend them on.

Gear upgrade tracks

  • Gear doesn't just have an item level — it sits on a track. Low to high: Adventurer (green), Veteran, Champion, Hero, Myth.
  • A tooltip reading Hero 3/6 means: the Hero track, rank 3 of 6. Each rank you buy adds item level.
  • The track sets your ceiling — the tooltip shows the item level range it can reach. To climb past it you need a drop from a higher track.
  • Each track has its own crest colour. Harder content drops gear on a higher track — that's how you climb.
  • Outgrown a track? Trade lower crests up at Vaskarn. Upgrade at Cuzoth in Silvermoon — a rank costs that track's crests plus a little gold.
  • Each track has an achievement for outgrowing it, named after the season's own crest — in Season 2 the «…of the Mist» line. Open it in your Achievements pane to see what it unlocks; the reward changed with the season and we would rather send you to the game than repeat last season's.
  • Live counts and waypoints: Basics -> Crests.
