Weekly reset

  • Most weekly progress resets on your region's maintenance day (EU Wednesday morning, US Tuesday morning).
  • Great Vault choices, world boss loot, many weekly caps, and Delver's Call deliveries reset.
  • Home -> This Week shows time until reset when the API provides it.
  • Plan alts: snapshot tab compares who still owes vault, boss, or Delver's Call.

Great Vault

  • Three tracks: World (delves + world content), Dungeons (M+), Raid.
  • Fill activities during the week; after reset you pick one reward per unlocked slot at the vault NPC or via SHIFT-J.
  • Vault Advisor (on Blizzard's vault UI) ranks options vs your equipped gear.
  • This addon shows all three tracks in the Delves & Vault tab — expand Weekly Great Vault (World) (not a separate tab yet).
  • Summary also on Home -> This Week and Account snapshot.

Delver's Call

  • Weekly delve objectives that grant a large XP burst when turned in.
  • You can bank completed calls (done objectives, not yet turned in) for a later character level.
  • Account snapshot rolls up banked and pending calls across alts.
  • Hover the Delver's Call line in snapshot for per-character detail.

Account snapshot

  • Read-only overview of characters you've logged in on with Midnight Helper.
  • Sort/filter by vault ready, keys, Shards of Dundun cap, stale since reset, etc.
  • Use it to answer: \"Which alt still needs vault / boss / Delver's Call?\"
  • Does not replace logging onto an alt for gear or quest checks.

Omnium Folio & Runes (12.0.7)

  • New power system that takes no gear slot. Unlock it via the Sunstrider Omnium questline with Magister Umbric and Grand Magister Rommath on Quel'Danas. Each week the 'Seeking Knowledge' quest grants a Mote of Omnial Inquiry to unlock one of five rune rows; runes swap freely out of combat.

Turbulent Timeways V (Jun 30 - Aug 11)

  • Timewalking marathon featuring six Dragonflight dungeons. Run four dungeons in a week for that week's Mastery buff; earn Mastery in four separate weeks to claim the Spawn of Vyranoth mount.

Omnium Folio (12.0.7)

  • New mid-expansion power system — a minimap book of runes you swap freely out of combat (no slot cost).
  • Power comes from the weekly 'Seeking Knowledge' chain (5 weeks): The Omnium Folio, Ritualized Arcana, Leyline Assaults, Magical Primessence, Off-World Magic.
  • Each week rewards a Mote of Omnial Inquiry to choose/empower a rune.
  • Finish all 5 for the meta achievement and the Sunstrider Omnium Simulacrum decor.
  • Missed a week? Do the missed quests back-to-back to catch up — no waiting for resets.
  • (Datamined — exact rune effects/IDs confirmed at launch.)
