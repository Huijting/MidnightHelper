Your gear says "Champion 4/6", your Great Vault has three empty slots, and the game never explains either.
**Midnight Helper answers those questions in plain language, in your own language, and then takes you there.**

For players who just hit 90 or are coming back after a break. Up to date for patch 12.1.5 and Season 2, and ready for
12.1.7. Free, no ads, [open source](https://github.com/Huijting/MidnightHelper), eight languages, no other addons needed.

### What it does

- **This Week**: the one page you need: what to do now, with a *Take me there* button. Mostly solo? One switch puts
  delves first.
- **How you play**: all 40 specs on one short card each, with the key every spell is on for you, a quiz, and every
  line checked against the game data and current guides. Healers get a **Clicks** tab: a ready-made click-healing
  layout, explained, that one button puts in DandersFrames or in Blizzard's own Click Casting.
- **Your gear and Great Vault**: what your upgrade tracks mean, which vault choice is best for you, and *My gear: what
  now?*: what you can upgrade right now with the crests you hold, and the next step up, in small steps. Next to your
  character window, every enchant in colour: ours, another one, or missing, with a shopping list for what you lack.
- **Delves, dungeons and raids**: routes, boss tips and coaches that open by themselves, for all 15 delves. In the
  Labyrinth of Kindo'jan a small window opens for every room and every boss.
- **Corrosive Codex**: which gifts to pick for bosses, packs or staying alive, lit up right in Blizzard's window.
- **Your keys**: your key block (the same job on the same key on every character, with one Undo), which spells you
  really press, and your whole keyboard, in one window.
- **Professions, raid shopping lists and the Role Academy** for learning to tank or heal.

### It takes you there, from anywhere

Type where you want to go in the search bar (a city, a delve, a dungeon or a raid) and pick **take me there**. Every
route starts where you stand and takes the fastest way: your Hearthstone (or a toy when it is on cooldown), a mage
teleport, the right portal, then step by step through every portal, boat, door and orb, into the old world too. Use
TomTom? It keeps the arrow.

### The Mount run

More than 40 old raids and dungeons you can do on your own for a mount, in one window: which mounts you still miss,
and which ones you can still try this week. Click **Route** and the arrow takes you to the entrance, including the
quest that opens a portal when you do not have it yet. Open it with `/mh mountrun`, from Tools, or on the Mounts tab.

### Everything else is on the website

**[midnighthelper.com](https://midnighthelper.com/)**: every play card, the whole Codex, the patch 12.1.5 page, the
Role Academy, guides for your week, delves and Mythic+, and an Armory gear check. Free, no account, eight languages.

New: every spec page has a **[Your talents](https://midnighthelper.com/play/)** tab. For **DPS**, **tanks** and
**healers** it shows which talents your play card counts on, how many of the 50 best Mythic+ players of your spec take
each one, and what changes for you if you don't have it.

### Getting started

Install it, log in and type `/mh`. Nothing in your game changes unless you press a button, and anything it sets up
can be undone. More commands: `/mh play`, `/mh mykeys`, `/mh mountrun`, `/mh ready`, `/mh academy`, and about 70 more
under **Tools**.

### Languages

English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (BR), Italiano, Русский and Nederlands (`/mh lang nl`). Game words follow
your own client.

### Help and feedback

A one-person project, checked in the live game before every release. Found a bug? Type `/mh report` in game, then
come to [Discord](https://discord.gg/kBHaHcsASQ) or use the [feedback form](https://midnighthelper.com/feedback/).
Want new things sooner? Set Midnight Helper to **Beta** in the CurseForge app.
