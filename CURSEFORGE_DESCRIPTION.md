[![Midnight Helper: plain-language help for WoW Midnight and WoW Forever. Guides, gear advice and feedback at midnighthelper.com](https://midnighthelper.com/cf-banner.png)](https://midnighthelper.com/)

**One addon for two games: WoW Midnight (Retail) and WoW Forever.** It answers the questions the game never explains,
in plain language and in your own language, and then takes you there.

Free, no ads, [open source](https://github.com/Huijting/MidnightHelper), eight languages, no other addons needed.
Up to date for Midnight patch 12.1.5 and Season 2 (ready for 12.1.7), and for WoW Forever 1.60.

### 🛡️ Playing WoW Forever? This is for you too

Despite the name, Midnight Helper has its own help for **WoW Forever**. In the CurseForge app, switch the game from
Retail to **Forever** and install it there as well. You get help made for levelling the way it used to be:

- **One overview for everything** (`/mh`, Alt+M or the minimap button): seven tabs. *Now* (what to do now and next),
  *Your class* (how you play, your class quests, camping), *Find*, *Professions*, *Dungeons & group*, *Keys & macros*
  and *Characters*. The gear button lists everything MH does by itself, each with a switch.
- **A boss guide for all 28 dungeons**, because WoW Forever has no Dungeon Journal: who the bosses are, what they do,
  one tip per boss and the items worth knowing. Hover a boss to see him in 3D; target one in a dungeon and a small
  card shows his tip.
- **Your trainer**: what you can learn right now and what it costs, your money, and **Take me to my trainer**: an arrow
  to your nearest class trainer who can teach you something new (218 trainers known, no other addon needed). Talk to a
  trainer or vendor once and the arrow learns exactly where they stand.
- **Search and Find**: type a trainer, shop, dungeon, class quest or place in the search bar. *Find* lists trainers,
  weapon masters, shops, the bank, the auction house, mailboxes, inns, zeppelins, boats, flight masters and the rares
  worth the trip. Nearest first, with the way there and the arrow.
- **Class quests for all nine classes**, step by step with the arrow: from the level 1 note that sends you to your
  trainer to druid forms, warlock demons, shaman totems, hunter pets, weapon quests and more. *Do this now* tells you
  what to bring before the trip, and a card you can move shows the step you are on, in your class colour.
- **Which quests before a dungeon?** A *Quests* button per dungeon lists what to pick up first and from whom, with the
  arrow where the place is known. Have the QuestieDB addon? Midnight Helper then uses its places where it has none of
  its own, and tells you in *Do this now* when a quest is ready to turn in.
- **Where do I get this recipe or ingredient?** The tooltip and *Skill up* say it where Midnight Helper knows, and say
  so when the answer may be old Classic data. *Skill up* also shows what to make for your next skill point.
- **Your next talent point**: for all nine classes, in the directions it has an order for, *Do this now* names the
  talent to take.
- **Bags full?** A small window shows what can go, and never suggests your food, ammunition, class reagents, potions,
  recipes, pets or mounts. *Destroy* always asks first.
- **XP per hour** and the time to your next level, under the XP bar of the overview.
- **The game's own tooltips everywhere**: hover a spell, item, tool or boss loot in MH for the same tooltip as in your
  bags or spellbook.
- **Do this now**: a small window with what you can do right now (new spells at your trainer, an old rank on your
  bars, a weapon to learn, a missing tool) and the trainer spells of your next 3 levels. Upgrades in your bags come
  first, with *Wear it* and *Not this one*. Next to your money you see your free bag slots, and a button opens *How you
  play*.
- **Which way do you want to go?** Pick one of your class's three talent trees, each in one plain sentence. *How you
  play* then tells you what that way needs: your armour, the weapon guides name, and which spells need a shield.
- **Your characters**: all your characters in one window, with level, gold (and the total), professions, and how many
  spells wait for them at the trainer.
- **The arrow knows the way across the water**: boats, zeppelins, the Deeprun Tram and the portals, step by step. No
  Hearthstone in your bags? It warns you before you cross.
- **Weapon and profession trainers**: which weapons you may learn and where (`/mh weapons`), an arrow to the trainer
  for your profession, and a card per profession: what it does, the tool it needs and where to buy it.
- **Where do I buy this?** An arrow to the nearest vendor for tools and trade goods. Forgot your Skinning Knife? MH
  names the nearest vendor the moment the game says you need it. Ingredients say in their tooltip which profession
  and which recipes use them.
- **Handy macros**: the top macros for your class and spec, ready to make with one click.
- **New at your trainer**: when you level up, a window shows every new spell with its rank, its price and the key it
  lands on. Hover a spell for its tooltip; your nearest trainer stands beside the list as a turning 3D figure.
- **How you play, for all nine classes**: what your class does, your buttons for levels 1 to 10, a simple order to
  press them, what comes later, and the mistakes beginners make. It names the keys of your own key block.
- **A key block for every class**: the same task on the same key on every character. A new character gets your bar
  layout with one click. Choose the old block, or **12 x 4**: the same 12 keys 1-4, Q E R F, Z X C V on every bar
  (then with Shift, Ctrl and Alt), where E is always your interrupt and Ctrl holds your bandage, potions, healthstone
  and trinket. *Why these keys?* explains every key, and the *Layout* button switches between the two. If you want, 5
  becomes *Interact With Target* and Shift-Space calls your fastest mount. The buttons show what the key does right
  now, also in Bear Form or Cat Form.
- **For enchanters**: a line in the tooltip and a small icon in your bags (Blizzard's bags and Baganator) on every item
  you can disenchant. Hold **Ctrl+Shift** and click it to disenchant it, without looking for the spell first.
- **Group check**: at a ready check a window shows who misses a class buff (and who can give it), plus your own
  elixir, food, weapon buff, potions and healthstone. Click a buff you can give to cast it.
- **Which quest reward to take**: MH marks the reward that is best for your class, or the one that sells for the most.
  Hover any piece of gear and a green line tells you why it beats what you wear: *+3 Strength (main stats for a
  Paladin)*.
- **You picked up an upgrade**: when better gear lands in your bags, a short message on your screen says so. No more
  keeping your bags open.
- **Train all** under the trainer window, a **cross above your trainer** when the arrow brings you there, and spells
  that come from a class quest marked as such. A button that still casts an old rank gets a calm orange edge.
- **Missing buffs, in words**: *Blessing of Might is not on you. Click the icon to cast it.* One click casts it on you
  in your highest rank, whatever you target, and small icons show the other buffs you miss. It also warns when a buff
  runs out soon, but not while a stronger one from another player is still on you.
- **A small group panel** (off until you turn it on): click a player to target them, right-click to cast your dispel
  on them.
- **Warnings you would otherwise notice too late**: only 2 free bag slots left (with your grey items to sell), low mana
  or health before you pull, and before you sell your last Mining Pick or Skinning Knife.

### What it does in Midnight

Your gear says "Champion 4/6", your Great Vault has three empty slots, and the game never explains either. For players
who just hit 90 or are coming back after a break:

- **This Week**: the one page you need: what to do now, with a *Take me there* button. Mostly solo? One switch puts
  delves first.
- **How you play**: all 40 specs on one short card each, with the key every spell is on for you, a quiz, and every
  line checked against the game data and current guides. Healers get a **Clicks** tab: a ready-made click-healing
  layout, explained, that one button puts in DandersFrames or in Blizzard's own Click Casting, and a **Solo** tab for
  healing alone in a delve or the Labyrinth, with Valeera at your side.
- **Your gear and Great Vault**: what your upgrade tracks mean, which vault choice is best for you, and *My gear: what
  now?*: what you can upgrade right now with the crests you hold, and the next step up, in small steps. Next to your
  character window, every enchant in colour: ours, another one, or missing, with a shopping list for what you lack and
  a step-by-step guide to buying it (silver or gold) and putting it on.
- **Delves, dungeons and raids**: routes, boss tips and coaches that open by themselves, for all 15 delves. In the
  Labyrinth of Kindo'jan a small window opens for every room and every boss. Race your own best time there, room by
  room, with Labyrinth splits. The **Delve Log** keeps your records per Tier and tells you after every delve how you did.
- **Corrosive Codex**: which gifts to pick for bosses, packs or staying alive, lit up right in Blizzard's window.
- **Your keys**: your key block (the same job on the same key on every character, with one Undo), which spells you
  really press, and your whole keyboard, in one window. Choose the old block or **12 x 4**: the same 12 keys 1-4,
  Q E R F, Z X C V on every bar, the same job on the same key for every spec (healers have their big heal on C), and
  Ctrl for your combat potion, health potion, healthstone and trinkets. The *Layout* button switches between the two.
- **Professions, raid shopping lists and the Role Academy** for learning to tank or heal.
- **What fits in 20 minutes?** (`/mh time`): the delves and rituals that fit the time you have, from your own runs.
- **You picked up an upgrade**: when gear that clearly beats what you wear lands in your bags, a short message on your
  screen says so, and the missing-buff icon now names the buff and says what to do.
- **Top 5 macros per spec** with a *Make* button in the Macros tab, and Midnight ingredients that say in their tooltip
  which profession uses them.
- **Using MF Nameplates?** `/mh colors` explains every colour on your nameplates, read from your own MF settings (in
  WoW Forever too).

### Find anything, then it takes you there

The search bar at the top finds everything Midnight Helper has: every single setting, every rare, treasure, mount
and play card, the bosses, and places in Silvermoon (type **ah**, **bank** or **mail**). A typo is fine, and words that mean
the same work too, in every language.

Type where you want to go (a city, a delve, a dungeon or a raid) and pick **take me there**. Every route starts where
you stand and takes the fastest way: your Hearthstone (or a toy when it is on cooldown), a mage teleport, the right
portal, then step by step through every portal, boat, door and orb, into the old world too. Use TomTom? It keeps the
arrow.

Not sure what a setting does? On the *All settings* page, the **Show me** magnifier next to many settings shows it to
you, right now.

### The Mount run

More than 40 old raids and dungeons you can do on your own for a mount, in one window: which mounts you still miss,
and which ones you can still try this week. Click **Route** and the arrow takes you to the entrance, including the
quest that opens a portal when you do not have it yet. Open it with `/mh mountrun`, from Tools, or on the Mounts tab.

### Everything else is on the website

**[midnighthelper.com](https://midnighthelper.com/)**: every play card, the whole Codex, the patch 12.1.5 page, the
Role Academy, guides for your week, delves and Mythic+, and an Armory gear check. Free, no account, eight languages.

New: every spec page has a **[Your talents](https://midnighthelper.com/play/)** tab. For **DPS**, **tanks** and
**healers** it shows which talents your play card counts on, how many of the 50 best Mythic+ players of your spec take
each one, and what changes for you if you don't have it.

### Getting started

Install it, log in and type `/mh`. Nothing in your game changes unless you press a button, and anything it sets up
can be undone. A new character gets one small *Welcome* window with what to set up, and after an update the same
window lets you choose the new things you want, instead of a long list.
One **Move** switch shows every Midnight Helper part on your screen, so you can drag each one where you want it.
More commands: `/mh play`, `/mh mykeys`, `/mh mountrun`, `/mh ready`, `/mh academy`, and about 70 more
under **Tools**.

### Languages

English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (BR), Italiano, Русский and Nederlands (`/mh lang nl`). Game words follow
your own client.

### Help and feedback

A one-person project, tested in the live game by the maker. Found a bug? Type `/mh report` in game, then
come to [Discord](https://discord.gg/kBHaHcsASQ) or use the [feedback form](https://midnighthelper.com/feedback/).
Want new things sooner? Set Midnight Helper to **Beta** in the CurseForge app.
