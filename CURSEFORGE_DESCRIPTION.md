[![Midnight Helper: plain-language help for WoW Midnight and WoW Forever. Guides, gear advice and feedback at midnighthelper.com](https://midnighthelper.com/cf-banner.png)](https://midnighthelper.com/)

**One addon for two games: WoW Midnight (Retail) and WoW Forever.** It answers the questions the game never explains,
in plain language and in your own language, and then takes you there.

Free, no ads, [open source](https://github.com/Huijting/MidnightHelper), eight languages, no other addons needed.
Up to date for Midnight patch 12.1.5 and Season 2 (ready for 12.1.7), and for WoW Forever 1.60.

### 🛡️ Playing WoW Forever? This is for you too

Despite the name, Midnight Helper (MH) has its own help for **WoW Forever**. In the CurseForge app, switch the game
from Retail to **Forever** and install it there as well. You get help made for levelling the way it used to be.

#### Start here

- **One overview for everything** (`/mh`, Alt+M or the minimap button): seven tabs. *Now* (what to do now and next),
  *Your class* (how you play, your class quests, camping), *Find*, *Professions*, *Dungeons & group*, *Keys & macros*
  and *Characters*. The gear button lists everything MH does by itself, each with a switch. Hover a spell, item, tool
  or boss loot anywhere in MH for the game's own tooltip.
- **Do this now**: a small window with what you can do right now (new spells at your trainer, an old rank on your
  bars, a weapon to learn, a missing buff or tool) and the trainer spells of your next 3 levels. Upgrades in your bags
  come first, with *Wear it* and *Not this one*. Next to your money you see your free bag slots, and a button opens
  *How you play*.
- **Search, Find and the arrow**: type a trainer, shop, dungeon, class quest or place in the search bar. *Find* lists
  trainers, weapon masters, shops, the bank, the auction house, mailboxes, inns, vendors who repair, zeppelins, boats,
  flight masters and the rares worth the trip, nearest first. The arrow knows the way across the water too (boats,
  zeppelins, the Deeprun Tram and the portals) and warns you when your Hearthstone is not in your bags.

#### Your class

- **How you play, for all nine classes**: what your class does, your buttons for levels 1 to 10, a simple order to
  press them, what comes later, and the mistakes beginners make, with the keys of your own key block. Pick one of your
  three talent trees, each in one plain sentence: *How you play* then names your armour, your weapon and which spells
  need a shield, and *Do this now* names your next talent point.
- **Class quests for all nine classes**, step by step with the arrow: from the level 1 note that sends you to your
  trainer to druid forms, warlock demons, shaman totems, hunter pets, weapon quests and more. *Do this now* tells you
  what to bring before the trip, and a card you can move shows the step you are on, in your class colour.
- **Your trainer**: when you level up, the trainer page in the overview shows every new spell with its rank, its price
  and the key it lands on; *Show the way* points the arrow to your nearest class trainer who can teach you something
  (218 trainers known). At the trainer: *Train all*, a cross above your trainer, spells from a class quest marked as
  such, and *You just learned*, a short list that asks per spell: on your bars now, later or never. A button that still
  casts an old rank gets a calm orange edge. Which weapons you may learn and where: `/mh weapons`.

#### Keys, macros and buffs

- **A key block for every class**: the same task on the same key on every character, the old block or **12 x 4** (see
  *In both games* below). In Forever, Ctrl holds your bandage, health and mana potion, healthstone and trinket,
  Shift-Space can call your fastest mount, and the buttons show what the key does right now, also in Bear Form or Cat
  Form. A new character gets your bar layout with one click.
- **Missing buffs, in words**: *Blessing of Might is not on you. Click the icon to cast it.* One click casts it on you
  in your highest rank, whatever you target, and small icons show the other buffs you miss. It also warns when a buff
  runs out soon, but not while a stronger one from another player is still on you. Buffing others? Mage, Priest,
  Druid and Paladin can switch on a smart buff that buffs the player under your mouse, otherwise your target.
- **For enchanters**: a line in the tooltip and a small icon in your bags (Blizzard's bags and Baganator) on every item
  you can disenchant. Hold **Ctrl+Shift** and click it to disenchant it, without looking for the spell first.

#### Gear, bags and professions

- **Which quest reward to take**: MH marks the reward that is best for your class, or the one that sells for the most.
  Hover any piece of gear and a green line tells you why it beats what you wear: *+3 Strength (main stats for a
  Paladin)*. Other tooltip lines say when your class can never use an item, and when a quest of yours needs it.
- **Bags full?** A small window shows what can go, and never suggests your food, ammunition, class reagents, potions,
  recipes, pets or mounts. *Destroy* always asks first. At a vendor, *MH: sell junk* sells your grey items. MH also
  warns you in time: only 2 free bag slots left, low mana or health before you pull, worn gear (with the nearest
  vendor who repairs), and before you sell your last Mining Pick or Skinning Knife.
- **Professions**: where you get a recipe or an ingredient (and a warning when the answer may be old Classic data),
  what to make for your next skill point, an arrow to your profession trainer, and a card per profession: what it
  does, the tool it needs and where to buy it. Forgot your Skinning Knife? MH names the nearest vendor the moment the
  game says you need it.

#### Dungeons and groups

- **A boss guide for all 28 dungeons**, because WoW Forever has no Dungeon Journal: who the bosses are, what they do,
  one tip per boss and the items worth knowing. Hover a boss to see him in 3D; target one in a dungeon and a small
  card shows his tip.
- **Dungeons & group**: which dungeons fit your level, the way to their entrance, your raid lockouts, and a *Quests*
  button per dungeon with what to pick up first and from whom. Have the QuestieDB addon? MH then uses its places where
  it has none of its own. MH warns you when nobody gets XP because someone in the group is more than 12 levels above
  the enemies.
- **Group check**: at a ready check a window shows who misses a class buff (and who can give it), plus your own
  elixir, food, weapon buff, potions and healthstone. Click a buff you can give to cast it. A small group panel (off
  until you turn it on): click a player to target them, right-click to cast your dispel on them.

#### Your account

- **Your characters**: all your characters in one window, with level, gold (and the total), professions and how many
  spells wait for them at the trainer, plus your reputations and currencies and what is on this week.
- **XP per hour** and the time to your next level, under the XP bar of the overview. Log out outside an inn and MH
  shows the nearest place to rest, so your rested XP builds faster.

### In both games

- **Key block 12 x 4**: the same 12 keys 1-4, Q E R F, Z X C V on every bar (then with Shift, Ctrl and Alt), with the
  same job on the same key for every class and spec. E is your interrupt (or the nearest thing your class has), Z X C
  your defensives, V crowd control. *Why these keys?* explains every key, and the *Layout* button switches between the
  old block and 12 x 4. If you want, 5 becomes *Interact With Target* and Shift-Space calls your mount.
- **You picked up an upgrade**: when gear that clearly beats what you wear lands in your bags, a short message on your
  screen says so.
- **Top macros per class and spec**, ready to make with one click.

### What it does in Midnight

Your gear says "Champion 4/6", your Great Vault has three empty slots, and the game never explains either. For players
who just hit 90 or are coming back after a break:

- **This Week**: the one page you need: what to do now, with a *Take me there* button. Mostly solo? One switch puts
  delves first. Hit level 90 and a small window shows where to go next.
- **How you play**: all 40 specs on one short card each, with the key every spell is on for you, how to stay alive,
  your dispels, your job in a group, and a quiz, every line checked against the game data and current guides. Healers
  get a **Clicks** tab: a ready-made click-healing layout, explained, that one button puts in DandersFrames or in
  Blizzard's own Click Casting, and a **Solo** tab for healing alone in a delve or the Labyrinth, with Valeera at your
  side.
- **Your gear and Great Vault**: what your upgrade tracks mean, which vault choice is best for you, and *My gear: what
  now?*: what you can upgrade right now with the crests you hold, and the next step up, in small steps. Next to your
  character window, every enchant in colour: ours, another one, or missing, with a shopping list for what you lack and
  a step-by-step guide to buying it (silver or gold) and putting it on. Want the exact best set? `/mh export` for the
  Armory on midnighthelper.com, `/mh raidbots` for Raidbots Top Gear.
- **Delves, dungeons and raids**: routes, boss tips and coaches that open by themselves, for all 15 delves, and a floor
  plan of the dungeon or raid you are in (`/mh map`). In the Labyrinth of Kindo'jan a small window opens for every room
  and every boss. Race your own best time there, room by room, with Labyrinth splits. The **Delve Log** keeps your
  records per Tier and tells you after every delve how you did.
- **In a group**: see what every group member is attacking, the Fast Mark bar for raid icons and world markers, a
  prompt when your interrupt or purge applies, and your battle res and Hero at a glance.
- **Corrosive Codex**: which gifts to pick for bosses, packs or staying alive, lit up right in Blizzard's window.
- **Your keys**: your key block (the same job on the same key on every character, with one Undo), which spells you
  really press, and your whole keyboard, in one window. In 12 x 4 healers keep their normal heals on 1, 2 and 3 and
  their emergency heal on C, and Ctrl holds your combat potion, health potion, healthstone and trinkets.
- **Missing buffs, in words**: the missing-buff icon names the buff and says what to do.
- **Rares, treasures and achievements**: the rares and treasures of your zone, and every Midnight achievement you still
  miss, by group (`/mh achlist`). All your characters at a glance in the account snapshot.
- **Professions, raid shopping lists and the Role Academy** for learning to tank or heal.
- **What fits in 20 minutes?** (`/mh time`): the delves and rituals that fit the time you have, from your own runs.
- Midnight ingredients say in their tooltip which profession uses them.
- **Using MF Nameplates?** `/mh colors` explains every colour on your nameplates, read from your own MF settings (in
  WoW Forever too).

### Midnight: find anything, then it takes you there

The search bar at the top finds everything Midnight Helper has: every single setting, every rare, treasure, mount
and play card, the bosses, and places in Silvermoon (type **ah**, **bank** or **mail**). A typo is fine, and words that mean
the same work too, in every language.

Type where you want to go (a city, a delve, a dungeon or a raid) and pick **take me there**. Every route starts where
you stand and takes the fastest way: your Hearthstone (or a toy when it is on cooldown), a mage teleport, the right
portal, then step by step through every portal, boat, door and orb, into the old world too. Use TomTom? It keeps the
arrow.

Not sure what a setting does? On the *All settings* page, the **Show me** magnifier next to many settings shows it to
you, right now.

### The Mount run

More than 40 old raids and dungeons you can do on your own for a mount, in one window: which mounts you still miss,
and which ones you can still try this week. Click **Route** and the arrow takes you to the entrance, including the
quest that opens a portal when you do not have it yet. Open it with `/mh mountrun`, from Tools, or on the Mounts tab.

### Everything else is on the website

**[midnighthelper.com](https://midnighthelper.com/)**: every play card, the whole Codex, the patch 12.1.5 page, the
Role Academy, guides for your week, delves and Mythic+, and an Armory gear check. Free, no account, eight languages.

Every spec page has a **[Your talents](https://midnighthelper.com/play/)** tab. For **DPS**, **tanks** and
**healers** it shows which talents your play card counts on, how many of the 50 best Mythic+ players of your spec take
each one, and what changes for you if you don't have it.

### Getting started

Install it, log in and type `/mh`. Nothing in your game changes unless you press a button, and anything it sets up
can be undone. A new character gets one small *Welcome* window with what to set up, and after an update the same
window lets you choose the new things you want, instead of a long list.
One **Move** switch shows every Midnight Helper part on your screen, so you can drag each one where you want it.
A few commands: `/mh play`, `/mh mykeys`, `/mh mountrun`, `/mh ready`, `/mh academy`. Every command is in **All
commands** (in Midnight under **Tools**, in Forever behind the gear button).

### Languages

English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (BR), Italiano, Русский and Nederlands (`/mh lang nl`). Game words follow
