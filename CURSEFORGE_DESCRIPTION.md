**One addon for two games: WoW Midnight (Retail) and WoW Forever.** It answers the questions the game never explains,
in plain language and in your own language, and then takes you there.

Free, no ads, [open source](https://github.com/Huijting/MidnightHelper), eight languages, no other addons needed.
Up to date for Midnight patch 12.1.5 and Season 2 (ready for 12.1.7), and for WoW Forever 1.60.

### 🛡️ Playing WoW Forever? This is for you too

Despite the name, Midnight Helper has its own help for **WoW Forever**. In the CurseForge app, switch the game from
Retail to **Forever** and install it there as well. You get help made for levelling the way it used to be:

- **Your own start page**: your level, what you can learn at your trainer right now and what it costs, your money, and
  **Take me to my trainer**: an arrow to your nearest class trainer (218 trainers known, no other addon needed).
- **New at your trainer**: when you level up, a window shows every new spell with its rank, its price and the key it
  lands on. Hover a spell for its tooltip; your nearest trainer stands beside the list as a turning 3D figure.
- **How you play, for all nine classes**: what your class does, your buttons for levels 1 to 10, a simple order to
  press them, what comes later, and the mistakes beginners make.
- **A key block for every class**: the same task on the same key on every character. A new character gets your bar
  layout with one click.
- **Which quest reward to take**: MH marks the reward that is best for your class, or the one that sells for the most.

### What it does in Midnight

Your gear says "Champion 4/6", your Great Vault has three empty slots, and the game never explains either. For players
who just hit 90 or are coming back after a break:

- **This Week**: the one page you need: what to do now, with a *Take me there* button. Mostly solo? One switch puts
  delves first.
- **How you play**: all 40 specs on one short card each, with the key every spell is on for you, a quiz, and every
  line checked against the game data and current guides. Healers get a **Clicks** tab: a ready-made click-healing
  layout, explained, that one button puts in DandersFrames or in Blizzard's own Click Casting, and a **Solo** tab for
  healing alone in a delve or the Labyrinth, with Valeera at your side.
- **Your gear and Great Vault**: what your upgrade tracks mean, which vault choice is best for you, and *My gear: what
  now?*: what you can upgrade right now with the crests you hold, and the next step up, in small steps. Next to your
  character window, every enchant in colour: ours, another one, or missing, with a shopping list for what you lack and
  a step-by-step guide to buying it (silver or gold) and putting it on.
- **Delves, dungeons and raids**: routes, boss tips and coaches that open by themselves, for all 15 delves. In the
  Labyrinth of Kindo'jan a small window opens for every room and every boss. Race your own best time there, room by
  room, with Labyrinth splits. The **Delve Log** keeps your records per Tier and tells you after every delve how you did.
- **Corrosive Codex**: which gifts to pick for bosses, packs or staying alive, lit up right in Blizzard's window.
- **Your keys**: your key block (the same job on the same key on every character, with one Undo), which spells you
  really press, and your whole keyboard, in one window.
- **Professions, raid shopping lists and the Role Academy** for learning to tank or heal.
- **What fits in 20 minutes?** (`/mh time`): the delves and rituals that fit the time you have, from your own runs.

### Find anything, then it takes you there

The search bar at the top finds everything Midnight Helper has: every single setting, every rare, treasure, mount
and play card, the bosses, and places in Silvermoon (type **ah**, **bank** or **mail**). A typo is fine, and words that mean
the same work too, in every language.

Type where you want to go (a city, a delve, a dungeon or a raid) and pick **take me there**. Every route starts where
you stand and takes the fastest way: your Hearthstone (or a toy when it is on cooldown), a mage teleport, the right
portal, then step by step through every portal, boat, door and orb, into the old world too. Use TomTom? It keeps the
arrow.

Not sure what a setting does? On the *All settings* page, the **Show me** magnifier next to many settings shows it to
you, right now.

### The Mount run

More than 40 old raids and dungeons you can do on your own for a mount, in one window: which mounts you still miss,
and which ones you can still try this week. Click **Route** and the arrow takes you to the entrance, including the
quest that opens a portal when you do not have it yet. Open it with `/mh mountrun`, from Tools, or on the Mounts tab.

### Everything else is on the website

**[midnighthelper.com](https://midnighthelper.com/)**: every play card, the whole Codex, the patch 12.1.5 page, the
Role Academy, guides for your week, delves and Mythic+, and an Armory gear check. Free, no account, eight languages.

New: every spec page has a **[Your talents](https://midnighthelper.com/play/)** tab. For **DPS**, **tanks** and
**healers** it shows which talents your play card counts on, how many of the 50 best Mythic+ players of your spec take
each one, and what changes for you if you don't have it.

### Getting started

Install it, log in and type `/mh`. Nothing in your game changes unless you press a button, and anything it sets up
can be undone. More commands: `/mh play`, `/mh mykeys`, `/mh mountrun`, `/mh ready`, `/mh academy`, and about 70 more
under **Tools**.

### Languages

English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Português (BR), Italiano, Русский and Nederlands (`/mh lang nl`). Game words follow
your own client.

### Help and feedback

A one-person project, tested in the live game by the maker. Found a bug? Type `/mh report` in game, then
come to [Discord](https://discord.gg/kBHaHcsASQ) or use the [feedback form](https://midnighthelper.com/feedback/).
Want new things sooner? Set Midnight Helper to **Beta** in the CurseForge app.
