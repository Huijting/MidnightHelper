# Midnight Helper 4.4.0

## NEW: All your characters side by side

The Account snapshot can now show your characters as **columns**, one per character, with the facts under
headings you can fold:

- **Overview:** level (with XP while levelling), spec, item level, gold, rested XP and when you last played them.
- **This week:** the Great Vault for world, dungeons and raid, and your Coffer Key Shards.
- **Currencies:** the same list as the Currencies block, so the two never disagree.
- **Professions:** with the skill each character has.

**Click a name** for a card with the gear that character wears: icon, item, item level, and the normal item
tooltip when you hover. **Big window** opens everything nearly full screen, so you see all your characters at
once. A **Rows / Columns** button switches back to the old view; your choice is remembered.

Gold, rested XP, spec and gear appear for a character once you have logged in on it with this version.

## Two minutes for Midnight Helper?

Midnight Helper is made by one person who has no idea which parts you use and which you never touch. There is
now a short, anonymous survey on our website: seven questions, every one optional. A card on This Week and a
one-time question invite you to it, only after the addon has helped you with something. `/mh survey` always
shows the link. Thank you!

## Also in 4.4.0

- **The Twin Fangs:** the boss tip now explains the poison beam from the snake in the middle. The orbs spinning
  around her head show which way it turns: walk against it and cross it early, behind the beam is safe.
- **Ready for patch 12.1.5** (13 October US, 14 October EU), so the addon will not show as out of date.
- All new text is in the seven languages Midnight Helper speaks.

Something wrong? `/mh report` in game, a comment here, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.
