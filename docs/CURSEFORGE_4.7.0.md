# Midnight Helper 4.7.0: the key block

**This release is all about one thing: your new key block.** Type `/mh block` and follow the steps at the top of the
window. We are really happy with it, and we hope you will be too.

## NEW: your key block, now on your bars

The idea is simple: **the same job on the same key, on every character.** 1 is always your main button, E your
interrupt, Z your small defensive, Q your big cooldown. Switch from your Paladin to your Hunter, and your hands already
know where to go. No more searching your bars, no more "where did I put my kick on this one?".

In 4.6.0, `/mh block` was only a picture. Now Midnight Helper puts it on your bars for you:

- **Step by step.** A short guide on top of the window shows what to do next, with one button per step. Done steps
  get a green tick.
- **Your spells on bars 5, 6 and 7:** three blocks of 3 × 4 keys, plus block D on bar 4 for your own things (the Alt
  keys). Whatever stood in the way moves to block D; nothing is thrown away. A spell the block already holds is not
  kept twice.
- **Your bars as a block on screen**, through Edit Mode, in one press. If your spec uses Modern, Classic or a layout
  shared by all your characters, Midnight Helper makes a copy just for this character and spec, so your other
  characters notice nothing. After the reload the window opens again, so you can go on with the next step.
- **Undo** puts every button and every key back exactly as it was. It works per character and per spec.
- **New spells:** when you learn one, Midnight Helper asks where it goes. You can also let it do that by itself, or
  never. While the block is placed, the game no longer drops new spells onto bar 1.
- **Skyriding, vehicles and pet battles:** keys 1 to 5 press the game's own buttons while you ride.
- **Healers:** every spell on your play card gets a key, with your heals first on 1 to 4.
- **Mouse keys (optional):** 6 7 8 9 0 - on bar 8 as a 3 × 2 block, for mice with side buttons.
- **Cheat sheet:** a code you can paste on midnighthelper.com, to print your keys or keep them on your phone.

Nothing on your bars changes until you press a button, and everything can be undone. Happy with your own bars? Then
you do not have to use it.

## Your play card shows your keys

On **Your buttons** (`/mh play`), every spell name now shows the key it sits on, for example *Flash Heal [3]*. Healers
also get a short line on how to heal someone: click your friend's frame, then press the key.

## Stay alive: a few more buttons

- Priests with **Protective Light**: Flash Heal as a small defensive.
- Balance, Feral and Restoration Druids: **Bear Form** as a big emergency button.
- Feral: **Regrowth** when it is free and instant.
- Devastation Evokers with **Stretch Time**: Deep Breath as a big emergency button.

## Also new

- **German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian:** game terms such as the Great Vault, raids, professions and
  skyriding now use the words your own game client shows.
- **Fix:** the "buff an ally" reminder no longer stays on screen when your Beacon of Light sits on your Delve companion.
- **`/mh export`** now also sends each item's ID, so the Armory on midnighthelper.com can show Wowhead tooltips.

Something wrong? `/mh report` in game, a comment here, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.
