# Midnight Helper 3.3.0

Almost everything here is a correction — not bugs, but sentences that were true when they were written and had quietly stopped being true.

## Knowledge Points can be undone

The professions chapter said your points were permanent and could never be refunded. That stopped being true on 11 August. You get one reset per profession, from Theremis in Silvermoon City, and it takes back the recipes those points taught you as well. There is a route button to him now, and he shows up in the search.

## Engineering was sending you into a dead end

Our route put Recycling last. In Midnight, recycling is how you discover most of your recipes, and it stays switched off until points go into it — so anyone following our advice recycled, saw nothing happen, and reasonably concluded the feature was broken. It is the first step now. Leatherworking also stopped contradicting itself on one screen, where the route said one thing and the paragraph beside it said the opposite.

## Three slots that could never get advice

Rings, weapons and shoulders were quiet because of how our own table was built, not because nothing exists for them. Rings and weapons were filed by secondary stat, which put a critical-strike enchant and a primary-stat enchant permanently out of reach. Shoulders now carry the same Speed, Leech and Avoidance choice head and feet already had. Every identifier was read out of your own client.

## Refreshed, measured, or withdrawn

Seventeen flask and combat-potion picks were still the set from May, and there is now a checker that compares our picks against the guide we cite, so the next time this goes stale it goes stale loudly. Ritual tier item levels are back, read standing at the obelisk: 215, 231, 244, 259, 268, 275. Tier 6 exists and wants six challenges. Gone: two Codex crest claims that outlived their season, the Field Accolade prices, a Spark of Radiance belonging to the previous expansion, and a weekly the reset routine still sent you to. Raid boss names now come from the game's own journal.

## What is coming, and why it arrives in bursts

A much larger professions update is being written: beginner chapters on quality, the six profession stats, Concentration, work orders and making gold, all checked against this patch.

Midnight Helper is a one-man project. That is the honest reason updates land in bursts rather than on a schedule.
