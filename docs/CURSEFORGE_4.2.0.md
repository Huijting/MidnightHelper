# Midnight Helper 4.2.0

## NEW: Maps of every raid, dungeon and delve

**Lost your group after a wipe? Not sure where the next boss is?** Midnight Helper now shows the floor
plan of every raid, dungeon and delve, right from its Raids, Dungeons and Delves pages.

- **Every floor**, with a button per floor at the top.
- **The bosses are on the map**, with their name. Click a boss to open Midnight Helper's tips for it.
- **The ways between floors** (stairs, portals) are drawn with their target floor. Click one and the map
  jumps to that floor, so you can walk through a whole raid before you go in.
- **Where to find it:** the **Map** button under the animated bosses on the Raids and Dungeons pages, and
  a small map icon on every row of the Delves page.
- **Inside a dungeon or raid,** type `/mh map` and it opens the floor you are standing on.

The maps are the game's own, so Midnight Helper did not get any bigger for it, and they stay right after
a patch. One thing no addon can show: where your group is. Inside a dungeon or raid the game hides
everyone's position.

## How you play: your own keys, Consumables and Dispel

The How you play window (`/mh play`, or the gold button in the search bar) got three additions:

- **Your real keys on every icon.** Each spell shows the key it is on right now, like `1`, `Z` or `S-2`,
  read live from your action bars. Move a spell and the label follows. A grey dash means the spell is not
  on a key yet. (Blizzard's own action bars for now; bar addons come later.)
- **Consumables tab:** the best flask, potion, oil, rune and food for your spec, how many you carry, and
  the alternatives, without searching through a long list.
- **Dispel tab:** what you can remove from your group and what you can take off enemies, where it matters
  in this season's dungeons and raid, and which of Blizzard's own settings show it live.

## Also in 4.2.0

- **Worn gear warning:** when an item is badly worn, a big warning with a sound appears as you enter a
  dungeon, raid or delve, and on every ready check. Set the limit yourself (Settings, Dungeon help;
  30% by default), or turn it off.
- **Party targets:** the DISPEL word now sits on the name, where the right-click dispels. It used to sit
  on the enemy half, where the right-click purges.
- All new text is in the seven languages Midnight Helper speaks.

Something wrong on a map or a card? `/mh report` sends it our way.
