# 253 Beast Mastery Hunter

## 1-2. W lines (enUS + nl)

**PLAYCARD_253_W1** (S1: Bestial Wrath every ~30 s, Barbed Shot just before)
- enUS: `{SPELL:19574} makes you and your pets hit harder for a while, and it hands you an extra {SPELL:217200}. Fire one just before, so the extra one has room and is not lost.`
- nl: `{SPELL:19574} laat jou en je dieren een tijdje harder raken, en geeft je een extra {SPELL:217200}. Schiet er vlak ervoor één, dan heeft die extra plek en gaat hij niet verloren.`
- Mechanic:
  - Bestial Wrath: +20% damage for you and your pets for 15 s, plus a big pet hit. MEASURED (DB2-L 19574, 344572; IV-BM 4.1).
  - Scent of Blood (BM talent): Bestial Wrath grants 1 charge of Barbed Shot. MEASURED (DB2-L 193532 adds +1 to effect #2 of 19574).
  - Barbed Shot holds at most 2 charges. MEASURED (DB2-L 217200). A charge granted at 2/2 is lost. INFERRED from the normal charge cap. It fits the guides: Method says "Use Bestial Wrath when you have less than 1 charge of Barbed Shot" (M-BM), and Icy Veins says "Barbed Shot if Bestial Wrath is about to come off cooldown" (IV-BM, APL-BM `st` line 1). Both MEASURED.
  - The "about every 30 seconds" on the card: the base cooldown is 90 s, and The Beast Within (BM talent) takes 60 s off. MEASURED (DB2-L 19574, 231548). IV-BM and M-BM both say 30 s.

**PLAYCARD_253_W2** (S2: Kill Command whenever ready, a shot in between)
- enUS: `{SPELL:34026} sends your pet in for one of your biggest hits. Your other shots make the next Kill Command hit harder (Nature's Ally talent), but that bonus does not pile up, so two Kill Commands in a row waste it.`
- nl: `{SPELL:34026} stuurt je dier op de vijand af voor een van je grootste klappen. Je andere schoten maken de volgende Kill Command sterker (talent Nature's Ally), maar die bonus stapelt niet, dus twee Kill Commands achter elkaar verspillen hem.`
- Mechanic:
  - Nature's Ally is BM's apex talent at level 90 (row 11). In its third stage, Barbed Shot, Cobra Shot and Black Arrow make the next Kill Command deal 30% more damage. MEASURED (DB2-L 1273126, 1276720).
  - The buff does not stack. MEASURED (WAGO: no SpellAuraOptions row for 1276720).
  - Icy Veins: "never cast two Kill Commands back-to-back ... weave another ability in between". MEASURED (IV-BM 3.2).
  - APL-BM uses `kill_command,if=... buff.natures_ally.react | !apex.3`. MEASURED. That `apex.3` means "third apex stage" is INFERRED.

**PLAYCARD_253_W3** (S3: Barbed Shot before 2 charges)
- enUS: `{SPELL:217200} makes the enemy bleed and gives you Focus while it bleeds. Shooting again adds what was left of the old bleed to the new one, and at 2 charges it stops reloading, so keep using it.`
- nl: `{SPELL:217200} laat de vijand bloeden en geeft je Focus zolang hij bloedt. Schiet je opnieuw, dan komt wat er nog over was van de oude bloeding bij de nieuwe, en op 2 charges laadt hij niet meer bij, dus blijf hem gebruiken.`
