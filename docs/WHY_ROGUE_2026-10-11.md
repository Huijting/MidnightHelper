# ASSASSINATION ROGUE (259)

Card: 4 steps + AOE, 2 hero lines.

### W1 (S1: Vanish, Garrote, Deathmark + Kingsbane; Kingsbane alone in between)
**EN:** `{SPELL:360194} makes your bleeds and poisons hurt that enemy more, and every poison you land on it lands twice. {SPELL:385627} grows with every poison, so next to {SPELL:360194} it grows twice as fast. A {SPELL:703} right after {SPELL:1856} hits harder for as long as it bleeds.`
**NL:** `{SPELL:360194} laat je bloedingen en vergif die vijand harder raken, en elk vergif dat je op hem zet, landt twee keer. {SPELL:385627} groeit met elk vergif, dus naast {SPELL:360194} groeit hij twee keer zo snel. Een {SPELL:703} vlak na {SPELL:1856} raakt harder zolang hij bloedt.`
- Deathmark: "Damage dealt to the target by your Garrote, Rupture, and Lethal Poisons is increased by $s2%. Each time you apply a Lethal Poison to the target, apply it twice." 120 s cooldown, 16 s. DB2-L + wago.tools 360194. **MEASURED.**
- Kingsbane: "Each time you apply a Lethal Poison to a target affected by Kingsbane, Kingsbane damage increases by ..."; 60 s cooldown, 14 s. DB2-L 385627. **MEASURED.** "Twice as fast" in Deathmark: M-A "During Deathmark, poison applications are doubled, causing Kingsbane to ramp twice as fast"; WH-A same idea. **MEASURED.**
- Improved Garrote 381632 (talent): "Garrote deals 50% increased damage and has no cooldown when used from Stealth and for 6 sec after breaking Stealth" (392401 = 6 s). DB2-L. **MEASURED.** That the bleed keeps the bonus for its whole run: IV-A "Snapshotting" (bonus removed only when you overwrite it with an unempowered one), WH-A "use the full 6 seconds of Improved Garrote to extend the duration of your empowered Garrote", APL `garrote,if=... pmultiplier<=1`. **MEASURED (guides + APL);** in the game **INFERRED.**
- Vanish has no other job for this spec: M-A "its only purpose is to apply Improved Garrote"; WH-A "the only remaining interaction with Stealth in Midnight for Assassination". **MEASURED.**
- No numbers in the line on purpose (the Deathmark % and 50% are tuning).

**NL:** `{SPELL:703} en {SPELL:1943} zijn bloedingen: ze blijven de vijand vanzelf pijn doen. Op een vergiftigde vijand geven ze je ook Energy terug (talent Venomous Wounds), en met meer Combo Points duurt {SPELL:1943} langer.`

DB2-L. **MEASURED.** IV-A: "letting your bleeds drop entirely will make you lose out on Energy". **MEASURED.** Whether every build takes it: not measured, hence "(talent)".
