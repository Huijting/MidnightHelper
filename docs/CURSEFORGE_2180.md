# Midnight Helper 2.18.0 — Ready for Season 2, and honest about where it isn't

## What will hurt you, where you are standing

The Delve Coach now lists the things not to stand in: 173 hazards across 20 instances, covering every Midnight dungeon and delve as well as the lair and the raid. Every name is read from your own game, so it is already in your language.

It goes by where you actually are, never by the page you have open — the coach doubles as something you read in a city, and a hazard list is the one section that would be a lie there.

## Season 2 opens on your reset, not on a date

The old gate used a single timestamp, which would have unlocked Season 2 content about a day early across Europe. It now asks your own client when your region's reset falls. The lair tip named that same day and now names both.

## Two achievement hunts, including the parts that have no map pin

Soft Underbelly and Oppose the Foes join the list. They were held back because several targets had no coordinates — which turned out to be correct rather than missing: those only exist while a Temple Strike or an Incursion is running. They are checklists now, and the arrow steps over anything with nowhere to point.

## /mh keys

Four Altar of Corrosion nodes cannot be bought at any price; each needs an item found somewhere in the Vaults. This says which item opens which, and what the two powers do. Where two sources disagree, it shows the disagreement instead of quietly choosing.

## /mh plan

A first version: the whole way to your current target, as clickable steps. The arrow still walks only the first of them.

## Fixed

The dispel icon had never once appeared, and the flight banner had never named where it was taking you: the same one-line mistake in two files, invisible from outside. A build check now watches for it.

Also a Route button on a hunt with nowhere to go, question marks where a criterion's name belonged, and a Hearthstone offered to someone standing in a delve.
