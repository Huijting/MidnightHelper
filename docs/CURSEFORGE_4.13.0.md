# Midnight Helper 4.13.0: your shopping list step by step, and the Labyrinth in your own language

Want new things sooner and help test them? We put out betas regularly: set the release type of Midnight Helper in the
CurseForge app to **Beta**. Found something odd, or does everything just work? Tell us: `/mh report` in game, a comment
here on CurseForge, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.

## New: "How do I buy this?"

Midnight Helper already tells you which enchants, gems and consumables you are missing, and puts them on a shopping
list. But then you stand at the auction house: which of the two rows do you buy, and how do you get a gem into your ring?
A new window explains it in nine short steps.

- **Silver or gold?** Most crafted items come in two kinds. Only the small icon behind the name tells them apart (the
  window shows you both icons). Gold is always the stronger one.
- **Compare the price.** Silver is not always the cheaper one: some sellers count on players who click too fast. Is
  silver just as expensive, or more? Then certainly gold. Food and the Augment Rune come in one kind only.
- **Read the whole name**: a gold *Quick Peridot* is weaker than a silver *Flawless Quick Peridot*.
- **Then put it on**: an enchant (right-click the scroll, click the item), a gem (Shift + right-click the item, Apply) and
  weapon oil. Every gem fits every socket, and you can wear only one Eversong Diamond.

Open it with the **How do I buy this?** button above the shopping list, or with **How do I do this? Step by step** under
*Buy what's missing* beside your character window. The game words in it (Total Price, Buy, Apply, item names) are the
ones your own game client shows.

## Labyrinth of Kindo'jan

- Room names, Hexmasks, *Happy Hexmask Collector*, the Amani Headhunters and the Juggernaut now use the names your game
  client shows, in every language. Before, several of them were still English everywhere.
- **The Hexmask room hint now works on every client language.** Walk into one of the five mask rooms and Midnight Helper
  tells you a Hexmask can be there. Until now that only happened on an English client.

## Smaller things

- **Paladin, *Stay alive* tab:** does your health drop a little near training dummies? That is Blessing of Dawn: the
  talent takes over part of the damage of allies around you, and the game counts dummies too. A known bug, not
  dangerous. The line only shows when you have the talent.
- **Clicks tab, Restoration Shaman:** Ctrl + middle click (Nature's Swiftness) now says it is a buff on yourself, so
  nothing shows on the player you click, and that Riptide does not use it up.
- The *on the website* link in the Clicks tab now opens the Clicks part of the page directly.

The new texts in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Russian and Mexican Spanish are our own translation:
native speakers, corrections are very welcome.

Have fun!
