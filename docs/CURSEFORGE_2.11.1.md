# Midnight Helper 2.11.1

A correction. One thing was wrong, and it was wrong in a way that matters.

The crest pages said each colour has a weekly cap of roughly 100. There is no such cap.
The game reports no weekly limit on any of the five tiers, measured on 22 July, and the
claim was taken out of the English and Dutch guide text the same day.

It was not taken out everywhere. The Codex still said it in all seven languages, and the
crest guide still said it in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian. So for nine
days the addon told most of its players about a limit that does not exist, and anyone
reading it might reasonably have stopped earning crests for the week believing the rest
would not count.

Gone from every language now.

Nothing else changed. No new features, no behaviour changes, nothing to configure. If you
never opened the crest pages, this release changes nothing for you.

Midnight Helper is here to explain things correctly, and a wrong number in a guide is worse
than no guide at all. That is why this goes out on its own instead of waiting for the next
feature release.
