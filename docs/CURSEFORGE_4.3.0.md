# Midnight Helper 4.3.0

## NEW: Battle res & Hero panel

**Is there a battle res left? Is Hero ready? Who can even cast it?** In a group in a dungeon, delve or raid,
Midnight Helper now shows a small panel with the answers.

- **Battle res charges** the group shares, with the time to the next one. They count in a Mythic+ key and
  during a raid boss. In LFR there is no real limit, and the panel says so.
- **Hero:** ready, or how long until you can use it again.
- **Who can do it:** everyone in your group who can res in combat or give Hero, with the name of their own
  spell (Rebirth, Soulstone, Time Warp, Fury of the Aspects, and so on).
- **Your own buttons:** your battle res, Hero and normal res, with the key each one is on right now.
- **Hover** over the panel for a short explanation. The **X** hides it until your next dungeon.
- Settings, Dungeon help: show it only in Mythic+ keys and raids, or turn it off.

## NEW: Which gear should you wear?

Two ways to find out, both started from your character sheet:

- **Quick advice: the Armory on midnighthelper.com.** Type `/mh export` (or click *Quick advice: Armory
  website* next to your character sheet), copy the text and paste it on midnighthelper.com/armory. The site
  picks the best item for every slot from what you wear and what is in your bags. It keeps weapons as a pair,
  respects Unique-Equipped rings and never swaps out a trinket with a special effect. Nothing is uploaded:
  your browser reads the text.
- **The exact answer: Raidbots (test phase).** Type `/mh raidbots` (or click *Best set from your bags:
  Raidbots (test)*), copy the text and paste it on Raidbots Top Gear. Raidbots simulates every combination
  of the items you pick, so trinket effects and set bonuses count. The window shows the steps, and
  midnighthelper.com/raidbots explains them one by one. **This is new and still being tested: use it at your
  own risk.**

One thing no addon can do is copy for you: World of Warcraft does not allow it, so pressing Ctrl+C is yours.

## Also in 4.3.0

- **Macros:** Bubble Cancel is now in the list for Protection Paladins too. The cancel macros (bubble, Ice
  Block, Turtle, Hover) now warn about Blizzard's *Press and Hold Casting* setting: it fires such a macro
  twice, so your bubble drops straight away.
- **Codex, Crests:** the cap counts what you have earned this season and rises every week. It used to say
  it counted what you can hold.
- All new text is in the seven languages Midnight Helper speaks.

Something wrong on the panel or the gear advice? `/mh report` in game, or the feedback form on
midnighthelper.com.
