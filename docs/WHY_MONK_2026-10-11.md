## 5. Card errors (268)
- **B1 IDEA "your kicks bring the brews back"** — the brews come back through **Keg Smash (−3 s) and Tiger Palm (−1 s)**; Blackout Kick itself does not (only via Blackout Combo, +2 s on the next Keg Smash). **MEASURED** DB2 121253/100780/205523/196736; IV 10 Aug. Proposed: `… Drink brews to wash it away; {SPELL:121253} and {SPELL:100780} bring the brews back.` / nl `… {SPELL:121253} en {SPELL:100780} brengen de brews terug.` (IDEA is not in the quiz pool.)
- **B2 AOE "Spare Energy goes into SCK instead of TP, but not twice in a row"** — "not twice in a row" is Icy Veins' reason *for Press the Advantage* ("prevents you from gaining stacks of Press the Advantage"). Press the Advantage is a choice against Blackout Combo, and all three IV builds take Blackout Combo (**MEASURED** RB + DEC). With Blackout Combo, IV 10 Aug and WH 12 Aug (same author) say to keep spending Energy on the Blackout-Combo Tiger Palm even in AoE ("will rarely, if ever, cast Spinning Crane Kick" — WH, Master of Harmony; Shado-Pan: SCK "when there are no other abilities to press and Blackout Combo is not active"). ME 11 Aug AoE list ends on SCK (no Tiger Palm). SimC APL (20 Aug) never casts SCK. → guides disagree on SCK; none of them supports "not twice in a row" for the recommended build.
  Proposed: `Same buttons: {SPELL:121253}, {SPELL:115181} and {SPELL:325153} already hit the whole group. Keep using {SPELL:100780} after {SPELL:205523}; Energy left over after that can go into {SPELL:322729}.` / nl `Dezelfde knoppen: {SPELL:121253}, {SPELL:115181} en {SPELL:325153} raken de hele groep al. Blijf {SPELL:100780} na {SPELL:205523} gebruiken; Energy die daarna over is, kan in {SPELL:322729}.`
- **B3 MISTAKE "then Keg Smash is not ready"** — Keg Smash has its own cooldown; the problem is that you cannot pay its 40 Energy. **MEASURED** DB2 121253. Proposed: `… then you can't pay for {SPELL:121253} when it is ready, and your brews come back slower.` / nl `… dan kun je {SPELL:121253} niet betalen als hij klaar is, en komen je brews trager terug.`
- OK as written (**MEASURED**): S1, S3, S4; HERO1 (Shado-Pan: Wisdom of the Wall 1272821 = Breath of Fire launches Flurry Strikes during Niuzao; Sal'salabim's Strength 383697 = Keg Smash resets Breath of Fire; APL shado_pan list; ME + IV); HERO2 (Harmonic Surge 1270958: Celestial Brew/Infusion gives 2 charges of Potential Energy for 30 s, next Tiger Palm or Vivify deals damage and heals). "Best in dungeons" for Shado-Pan: IV 10 Aug says it excels in M+ but Master of Harmony is "just as effective".
