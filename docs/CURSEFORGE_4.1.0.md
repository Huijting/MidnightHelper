# Midnight Helper 4.1.0

## NEW: How you play — every spec explained, in one small window

**Midnight Helper now tells you how to play your spec.** All 40 specs have a short card, written so
that anyone gets it on the first read. No theorycrafting, no priority lists of twenty lines: just what
you press, in what order, and why.

**Open it with the gold "How you play" button** in Midnight Helper's search bar (it is there on every
tab, with the icon of your spec), or type `/mh play`.

Every card has the same short shape:

- **The idea** in one sentence: what your spec is about.
- **Your buttons, most important first:** four or five steps, each with the spell's icon.
- **More enemies:** what changes when you fight a pack.
- **Biggest mistake:** the one thing beginners get wrong.
- **Hero talents:** one line per hero talent tree, only what changes.

And it is made to be used while you play:

- **Point at any spell name** and you get that spell's tooltip, in your own game language.
- **Stay alive tab:** your defensives in the order a fight needs them, with your own keys.
- **Look at your other specs** with the icons at the top, before you switch.
- **Levelling?** The cards are written for level 90. On a lower character, the spells you do not have
  yet are grey, so you can see what is still coming.
- **A small window you can drag anywhere.** It stays where you put it, so you can keep it open next to
  a target dummy while you practise. Shift + mouse wheel makes it bigger or smaller.

The cards are written from this season's guides (Icy Veins, Method and Wowhead), and every card shows
the date of the guides it came from. A patch can change a rotation; the date tells you how fresh the
card is.

The cards are in all seven languages Midnight Helper speaks, and spell names always show the way your
own client writes them.

## Also in 4.1.0

- **Translations:** most of the last English lines in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian
  are translated now. Names Blizzard owns, like zones, bosses and currencies, stay as your client shows
  them.
- **Delves:** the Shadow Enclave text you share with your group also explains the Infiltrate and
  Ameliorate variant: kick 5 Summoners into the abyss, and put odd ingredients in 4 cauldrons.
- The Role Academy has a How you play button at the top of the tank, healer and DPS tracks.

Something wrong on your spec's card? `/mh report` sends it our way, and the card gets fixed.
