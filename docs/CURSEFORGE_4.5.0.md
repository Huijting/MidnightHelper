# Midnight Helper 4.5.0

## NEW: Mythic+, from your very first key

Never run a key, and no idea where to even sign up? There is now a guide for exactly that, in the Codex under
**Dungeons & M+** (type `/mh codex`):

- **Your first key:** where it comes from, and what to do if you have none.
- **Join a group:** where the Premade Groups are, which keys to pick as a beginner, and what the **Learning**
  playstyle means.
- **Start your own group:** listing your key, setting the difficulty, and the Font of Power.
- **During the run:** what a death costs (nothing on +2 to +5), what happens in time or too late, and how to stop
  without becoming a Keystone Deserter.
- **Help:** your guild, communities, and what Midnight Helper already does for you inside.

The button names in the guide come from **your own game client**, so a German or French player reads the words on
their own screen. `/mh mplus` points to the guide when you have no runs yet this season. The same guide is on our
website: midnighthelper.com/guides/mythic-plus/

## NEW: "I mostly play solo"

Asked for in our survey. A switch in the settings hides the **Mythic+** and **Raids** blocks from the weekly plan on
Home, so a solo player sees delves, world quests, professions and the Great Vault without the group content. A grey
line says what is hidden and where to turn it back on.

## Every tip checked again for Season 2

- **Raids:** the short tips of all 17 bosses were rewritten after a second check, and the long tips corrected where a
  source proved them wrong. Example: Lura on Heroic drops one pool, not two, since the June tuning.
- **Mythic+ dungeons:** all 8 Season 2 dungeons. Den of Nalorakk changed most: in 12.1 the big hits strike the whole
  group (certainly on Mythic), and Zul'jarra's shield takes off 80%, not half.
- **Delves and Valeera:** every delve checked against 12.1, with the new stories added. Fixed facts include what a
  Coffer Key opens (the Bountiful Coffer, not the delve), Delver's Call being once per character, and the Great
  Vault article.
- The delve tooltip no longer shows a **Speed Grade**. It was a placeholder, never a measurement, and it should not
  have been there.

Where a source could not settle something, the text stays as it was and we will check it in the game first. All new
text is in the seven languages Midnight Helper speaks.

Something wrong? `/mh report` in game, a comment here, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com. The survey now
has a box at the end for anything else you want to tell us. Thank you for the kind words so far!
