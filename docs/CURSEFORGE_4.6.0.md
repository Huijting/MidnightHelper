# Midnight Helper 4.6.0

## NEW: a "Group" tab on your play card

Open your play card with `/mh play`. Next to **Your buttons**, **Stay alive**, **Consumables** and **Dispel** there is
now a fifth tab: **Group**. It lists the buttons you have that help the whole group, such as a battle res, Bloodlust,
a raid-wide defensive or a speed boost, each with a short note on when to press it.

- Every spell on it was checked against its tooltip for Season 2.
- It only lists what **your** character has. Where a talent gives you one of two choices, you see the one you took.
- 34 of the 40 specs have one. Demon Hunter and Rogue have no tab yet: we found too little to fill it honestly.

## Your play card, checked again

- All 40 play cards were checked against current Season 2 guides. A few got new steps, for example the taunt on
  Protection Paladin and Prismatic Bolt on Arcane Mage.
- Every card now has a link to its own page on midnighthelper.com, so you can read it on your phone.
- **Stay alive** was checked row by row for every spec. Wrong entries are fixed or removed, a few missing defensives
  were added, and Warlocks now see their pet's interrupt (Spell Lock, Axe Toss) behind the Command Demon button where
  it really lives. Mirror Image only shows as a defensive if you have Refractive Images.

## This Week: shorter, to-dos first

The page had grown to about 70 rows, with only one in six something to do. Now:

- What you still have to do comes first. Finished stops fold into one line ("Done this week: 7"); click it to see them.
- Lines about your other characters moved to the **Account snapshot**.
- The weekly quest givers are fixed: Lady Liadrin no longer says "done" when she is still offering a quest,
  Lor'themar is gone (he has no weekly in Season 2), and **Zerella** (Sparks of War) and **Talon Commander Zela**
  (Coiled Isle) are new. Maella's Showdown weekly is back: it was never removed for Season 2.

## Also new

- **`/mh aggro`**: the game already warns you when an enemy targets you, but many players have those warnings off.
  This command shows which ones are on, and a new Codex chapter explains where each one is.
- **Settings:** the Delve Coach can stop opening by itself.
- Checked again for Season 2, in all seven languages: the Codex, currencies and crests, the Great Vault advice, the
  profession course (Shards of Dundun are now counted correctly), Ritual Sites, world bosses, achievements and
  consumables.

Something wrong? `/mh report` in game, a comment here, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.
