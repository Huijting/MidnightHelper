# Forever city services: bank, auction house, mailbox, inn (17 Nov 2026)

Research for the overview's Find tab (Forever only). Written by mh-research on 17 Nov 2026. Nothing here was seen in
game by Rob. Every claim says GEMETEN (seen in a named, dated source) or AFGELEID (reasoned).
The Lua block at the bottom is generated by a script from the sources themselves, not typed by hand.

## 1. Report

### Choices
- **Bank and auction house: one row per NPC** (per banker, per auctioneer), not per building.
- **Inn: one row per innkeeper NPC.** Dalaran has two inns, so two rows.
- **Row value for an NPC** = Zygor Forever when Zygor has that NPC on that map (project trust order: Rob measured >
  HandyNotes > Zygor > guide), else the Wowhead medoid. Wowhead is the cross-check on every row.
- **Row value for a mailbox** = Zygor's mailbox table when it has that box, else wowfdb.com. A mailbox is a live row only
  when at least two sources agree; a mailbox with one source is in the block but commented out (`-- ONE SOURCE:`).
- "Agrees" = within 1.0 map unit for an NPC, within 0.7 for a mailbox pin, within 2.0 for Wowhead mailbox objects
  (Wowhead's object points scatter around the box; that they are player positions is AFGELEID).

### Sources (all read 17 Nov 2026)

### The questions in the task
- **Valanaar.** GEMETEN: AreaTable 16638 "Valanaar", parent 16593 "Zephras Isle"; no own UiMap row, so it sits on
  uiMap 2521 (Zephras Isle, the Skyborne starting zone). UiMap has a second row named Zephras Isle, 2665, with
  System 1 and no parent; 2521 has System 0 and parent 947 and is the one Zygor uses. That 2665 is the flight-map
  copy is AFGELEID; which id the client reports while you stand in Valanaar is not measured. The handoff's "bank 57.6,77 and AH 57.8,77" is exact:
  banker Baelann Favorbreeze (257036) at 57.6,77.0 and auctioneer Zelena Favorbreeze (257037) at 57.8,77.0 in both
  Zygor and Wowhead; innkeeper Donaal Downbreeze (255940) at 62.2,72.6. Side: Zygor flags all three B (both); wowfdb
  prints no faction on them; warcraftforever.wiki and everythingwow.com say "Neutral". GEMETEN in those sources. One
  guide (wowforevertierlist.com, dated 8 Oct, beta) says the two factions are phased apart on the isle; that does not
  change the rows. So: it exists, it is neutral, and it has bank, auctioneer, inn and (wowfdb only) two mailboxes.
  NOT measured: which auction house Zelena opens (a faction house or the neutral one).
- **Dalaran** is the other city Forever adds. GEMETEN: AreaTable 279 "Dalaran", parent 36 Alterac Mountains; no own
  UiMap row, so uiMap 1416. Map 2959 "City of Dalaran" (InstanceType 1) is the dungeon, a different place. Two banks:
  east (Tellers Rames, Hanners, Althiellis, about 22.8,65) and west (Tellers Gee, Plushner, Almeida, Paymasters Amadi
  and Chang, about 10.6,68.6); two innkeepers (Isirami Fairwind 32413 at 12.2,66.5 and Amisi Azuregaze 28687 at
  18.2,66.5; Zygor's name list gives Amisi the id 96806, which Wowhead Forever does not know). Side A: Zygor flags
  three tellers and Isirami A, wowfdb prints "(Alliance)" on its three tellers and both innkeepers, urgametips says
  the city's services are for Alliance players and Horde are attacked, everythingwow says "Capital City, Alliance".
  AreaTable FactionGroupMask is 2 for Dalaran, the same value as Stormwind City (The Barrens has 4); that 2 means
  Alliance is AFGELEID. One guide (wowforeverinfo.com, 9 Nov) calls Dalaran's trainers neutral: that contradicts the
  others and is not taken over. **No auctioneer** is listed for Dalaran in Zygor's Auctioneer list or on wowfdb's
  Alterac page (both lists do show auctioneers elsewhere); that is absence in two lists, not proof. Mailboxes: 13 on
  wowfdb only, so all commented out.
- **The three neutral goblin towns.** GEMETEN in Zygor and Wowhead: Gadgetzan has 1 auctioneer, 2 bankers, an
  innkeeper and a mailbox. Everlook has 1 auctioneer, 1 banker, an innkeeper and a mailbox. Booty Bay has 3
  auctioneers, 2 bankers and an innkeeper; its mailbox (26.70,76.36) is in Zygor only, so it is commented out.
- **Auction house cut.** GEMETEN in Blizzard's own table (DB2 `AuctionHouse`, Forever build 1.60.1.70938): the Alliance
  and Horde houses have DepositRate 5 and ConsignmentRate 5; the "Blackwater Auction House" rows (FactionID 369 =
  "Gadgetzan" in the Faction table) have DepositRate 25 and ConsignmentRate 15. So "neutral: 15% instead of 5% and
  5x the deposit" matches the numbers exactly. That ConsignmentRate is the percentage kept from a sale and DepositRate
  scales the deposit is AFGELEID from the column names; conquestcapped.com (13 and 16 Nov), ahledger.com and wowfah.com
  read it the same way. NOT measured: a real sale in game. Reported by conquestcapped.com, not checked by me: the
  capital houses are not cross-faction (their in-game test of 16 Nov), and the minimum deposit drops from 1 silver to
  1 copper with the maintenance of 17 Nov (US) / 18 Nov (EU).
