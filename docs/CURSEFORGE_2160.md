# Midnight Helper 2.16.0 — Getting there, and knowing your keys

## A route for the chests inside a delve

The delve coach has a Chests button. It sends the arrow to the nearest Sturdy Chest you have not opened and moves on by itself once you loot it — 36 chests across 13 delve maps. Blizzard's own map pin cannot be placed inside a delve; the addon's arrow can, which is why this works at all.

It tells you when it is unsure. Where the flag that marks a chest as done cannot be read, the chest stays in the route instead of quietly vanishing: the cost is a chest you walk to twice, never one hidden from you.

## Travel that points at the flight master

Ask for a waypoint on another map and the arrow now goes to the flight master you can walk to, then hands over to the destination the moment you board. The flight map itself names the stop to click, and says so when that stop is not on your map yet.

Flight points went from 23 to 649 across 154 maps and respect your faction, so this works outside the Midnight zones too.

## /mh binds

A new command that reads the keys you actually have, including anything you changed by hand, and puts them in a window you can copy and print. It names the Assisted Combat slot for what it is rather than for whichever spell it suggests at that moment, and marks keys that duplicate another.

## Fixed

The Layout tab's right-hand column showed spells with no key beside them. The keys were there; they were being drawn past the edge of the panel. A missing key does not look broken — it looks like an ability with no bind.
