# Midnight Helper 2.15.0 — The Coiled Isle, for real

Patch 12.1 added two delves, and now they are really here: Gnarldor Isle and The Ring of Glory sit in the Delves panel, the coach and the picker — found on your own game client, not copied from a datamine. Each one has a route with its three Sturdy Chests as clickable waypoint links, its bosses with the two or three things a first-timer needs to know, and 3D boss models.

The Venomous Abyss raid coach is filled in ahead of the 18 August opening: beginner steps for all eight bosses with clickable spell links, tank and healer notes where the data was solid, and a strip of 3D boss models measured from the game's own Adventure Journal. A note above the tips says plainly that they were written before the raid opened, and they will be verified against the real fights once it does.

The Codex grew a Coiled Isle shelf. The Vaults of Atal'Utek wall of text became three readable articles — the zone itself, the Altar of Corrosion's four hidden keys, and The Honored Dead — and every coordinate in them is now a clickable waypoint. The Honored Dead article carries a Follow the route button that starts the same twelve-stop memorial route the Achievements tab runs. Coordinate clicks use the addon's own arrow: TomTom if you have it, otherwise a Blizzard pin with SuperTrack, plus travel advice for reaching the zone first.

Quality of life: a crest bundle you cannot open now explains itself before you click — the tooltip shows your season total and says that spending does not help, because the cap counts what you have earned and rises at the weekly reset. Bundles that are fully blocked step out of the Openables button instead of sitting in your way, and the list also reads the game's own has-loot flag now, so containers our patterns missed appear on their own.

Honesty notes, because that is the house style: Er'inye's key items are explained (showing him a key is a hint about where it belongs, not an unlock), where the keys drop is marked as genuinely unsettled, and the Codex no longer calls itself a Season 1 handbook three days before that stops being true.

Thanks for the reports this week — the stuck crest bundle, the delve picker overflowing its frame and the unclickable coordinates were all found by players within hours. Keep them coming.
