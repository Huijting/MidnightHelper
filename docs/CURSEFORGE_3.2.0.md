# Midnight Helper 3.2.0

This one is mostly about telling you the truth, including where the previous version did not.

## A correction we owed you

3.1.0 said that delve tiers 9, 10 and 11 hand out exactly what tier 8 does. That is true of the gear and false of the crests. Measured on a live client this week: a Tier 8 Bountiful run paid Champion Mistcrest, a Tier 11 run paid Hero Mistcrest. Climbing past 8 does buy you something after all, just not out of the chest. The delve reward panel now says so, and says which half was measured.

## You are not missing a health potion

Season 2 replaced Silvermoon Health Potion with the Concentrated version, and brewing the new one consumes 25 of the old. So everyone who upgraded no longer carried the item the consumables check looked for, and got a red cross at every dungeon door while holding the better potion. Both are recognised now.

## The portal to The Coiled Isle, both ways

The portal only exists once you are into the Prey line, so the pin greys itself out until you are — it is worth knowing it is coming rather than wondering why the wall is blank. The way in is a pin in the City Guide under Travel, on a coordinate measured standing at Astalor rather than copied from a guide, because the old one was nearly four points out. The way back has its own waypoint in the Prey guide. Every guide tells you how to reach an island and goes quiet about leaving it.

## Getting a gem into a socket

The Enchants tab told you which gem to use and never how to put it in. There is now a button that opens the socket window for the slot that needs one, and it appears only when you actually have an empty socket.

## Rares that need a word of warning

Coin-Eye Skully swims and has no fixed spot. Farthik only shows up once you open the chest. Both now say so in chat when you get close, rather than on our own arrow, which stays hidden for everyone running TomTom.

## Also fixed

The consumables board no longer opens itself in a raid, where it could only ever show five of forty. Travel advice no longer names an arbitrary flight point, and no longer calls a delve "another continent" from inside one. The Ring of Glory warns that the higher tiers send three loose golems, and that Gnok gets up again. The About window now credits the addons and guides we check our facts against.
