# Midnight Helper 5.5.0: one overview for WoW Forever, class quests for every class, and the bank in Find

Want new things sooner and help test them? We put out betas regularly: set the release type of Midnight Helper in the
CurseForge app to **Beta**. Found something odd, or does everything just work? Tell us: `/mh report` in game, a comment
here on CurseForge, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.

## WoW Forever: one window for everything

`/mh`, Alt+M, the minimap button and the quick bar now open the **overview** (or type `/mh overview`). It has seven
tabs. Hover over a tab or a button for a short explanation. The old commands still work and open the right tab.

- **Now**: what to do now, what comes next, your path and this week. A *Details* button opens your trainer page: your
  money, what each spell costs, and the key each spell gets on your key block.
- **Your class**: How you play, all your class quests (each with a button for the arrow), and camping.
- **Find**: trainers, profession trainers, weapon masters and shops on your side, nearest first, with the way there and
  *Show the way*. New: **In town**, with the bank, the auction house, mailboxes and inns. Neutral auction houses
  (Gadgetzan, Booty Bay, Everlook) are marked: they also trade with the other side, but keep 15% of the sale instead of
  5% and ask a bigger deposit.
- **Professions**: your professions with your skill, a card for each one, whether you carry its tool, and buttons to
  its trainers and to where you buy the tool. Three switches for the help around professions.
- **Dungeons & group**: every dungeon with its levels and the zone of its entrance, a switch *Only for my level*, your
  raid lockouts, the highest level in your group and the XP rule for groups. Horde players see that the Hall of Thanes
  has its entrance in Ironforge.
- **Keys & macros**: your key block, which spells you press, your keyboard, three switches (mouseover, the frame on an
  old rank, the MH bar layout) and the handy macros.
- **Characters**: all your characters with your total gold, your Legacy Points, and the whole week.
- **The gear button**: *Quiet helpers* lists everything Midnight Helper does by itself, each with one sentence and a
  switch. *Tools* opens every other Midnight Helper window. *All commands* lists every command, with a search box.

The old Midnight Helper window and the *Dock* button are gone in Forever. Right-click on the minimap button, or
`/mh settings`, opens Midnight Helper in the game's settings. The small windows (*Do this now*, *Your direction*, *New
in this version*, the key block) now have the same look as the overview.

## WoW Forever: more help while you level

- **Class quests for all nine classes**: *Do this now* leads you step by step, with the arrow and the names in your
  language. Druid forms, warlock demons and steeds, warrior stances, shaman totems, your first hunter pets, rogue
  poisons, the paladin's Redemption and charger, the priest's race spells, and the mage's quests.
- **How you play per role**: for all nine classes, a simple order to press your spells, what you get every 10 levels,
  and three beginner mistakes.
- **Ten new handy macros**: eat and drink in one press, Healthstone and Soulstone, mana gem, aspects, Prowl, Stealth,
  Vanish + Sprint, group buffs, resurrection, and Shackle Undead or Hibernate on your focus. The macros use your highest
  rank.
- **Free key**: does a Blizzard key (like Shift+5 for action page 5) take the Shift part of a macro? The macro row says
  so, with a *Free key* button. *Undo freed keys* puts it back. Keys of the MH key block and of other addons are never
  touched.
- **Camping**: near a campfire without the camp buff, *Do this now* tells you to sit down. After 1 minute you get Camp
  Benefits for 1 hour.
- **Legacy Points**: have unused points? *Do this now* says how many, and where you spend them.
- **Paladins: a blessing tip**: switch it on in the options (or `/mh blesstip on`). Hover a friendly player and the
  tooltip says which blessing fits their class.
- **Forever Leveling Guide**: while the guide runs a route, Midnight Helper sets no pin and draws no arrow. Want our
  arrow anyway? There is a switch in the options.

Have fun!
