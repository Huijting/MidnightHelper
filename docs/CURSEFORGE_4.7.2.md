# Midnight Helper 4.7.2: a complete key block for every class

The key block from 4.7.0 knew most of your spells, but not all of them. Some talents and class spells had no place, for
example Fury of Elune for Balance Druids or Shadowfury for Warlocks. This update fills those gaps.

## Every spell gets a key

- **About 100 spells added** across all 13 classes, checked against Blizzard's own game data for patch 12.1. A few
  examples: Shadowfury, Demonic Circle and Summon Doomguard (Warlock), Execution Sentence (Retribution), Reaper's Mark
  (Death Knight), Slice and Dice (Rogue), Fury of Elune and Force of Nature (Balance), Devastate (Protection Warrior).
- **About 20 old spells removed** that no longer exist in Midnight, such as Steel Trap, Living Bomb and Repentance.
- **Spells with no place on blocks A, B and C now go on a free Alt key in block D.** Mark of the Wild, Revive and
  similar buttons used to be left out. They never push anything off your main keys, and your own things on D stay where
  they are.
- **Spells Midnight Helper does not know yet** (for example after a patch) also go on a free Alt key, and the window
  tells you which ones they are.

**Placed the block before?** Open `/mh block`. Midnight Helper offers the new spells by itself; press **Place it**. Or
press **Undo** and place the block again.

## Smaller fixes for the key block

- No more double copies of your spells on block D.
- If several characters share one Edit Mode layout, the guide now offers each of them a layout of its own, so a change
  on one character no longer moves the bars of another.
- The play card shows the key from your key block, also for spells you have on two buttons.

## NEW: Ready for the raid?

Type `/mh ready`. A small window shows what you need for a raid night of about four hours, with what you carry and, in
red, how many to buy. Choose **Learning a new boss** or **Farm** at the top. Flasks and potions from a raid cauldron, and
the Warlock's Demonic Healthstone, now count too.

Something wrong? `/mh report` in game, a comment here, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.
