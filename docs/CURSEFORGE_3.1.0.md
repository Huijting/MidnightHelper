# Midnight Helper 3.1.0 — The nemesis delve, and what a tier is actually worth

## Venomfall Deeps

It was the one delve with no page at all. It has one now, and it opens in red: Azta'rec's Wrath of Ula'tek kills you from full health, your own death recap marks it avoidable, and it lands during the quadrant intermission — where being caught leaves you taking +500% Nature damage for the rest of the fight.

His abilities repeat in a fixed order, so you always know which one is the interrupt. The page also says plainly that this delve hands out no coordinates at all, so the route arrow going quiet in there is Blizzard's doing and not a broken addon.

## What each delve tier is worth

Every figure read off the entrance window rather than copied from anywhere: end chest and Trovehunter's Bounty for all eleven tiers, with the upgrade track beside each. The useful part is what it shows — tiers 9, 10 and 11 hand out exactly what tier 8 does. Climbing past 8 for loot buys you nothing.

The Great Vault column fills itself in as you play. Run a delve and your own vault reports what that tier was worth this week, marked with a star so it is never mistaken for a published table.

## Fixed, and it shipped in 3.0.0

German, Spanish, French and Portuguese were printing raw escape characters on screen: 362 places where a paragraph break appeared as the letters backslash-n, and 116 where every quotation mark carried a visible backslash. Almost all of it in the Role Academy, which is exactly the text a beginner reads most carefully. Spanish also showed an unfinished translation marker where a number belonged.

German additionally gained the whole DPS Academy track, five missing heal chapters and 49 dungeon boss tips — those tabs used to show German and English side by side in one window.

## Smaller things

The Codex button that said "Void & Rituals" now says "World content". Prey hunts, rares, Showdowns and Turbulent Timeways were all filed behind that name, which is a good way to own a page nobody finds.

Sixteen more hazards, and the Coiled Isle portal is now explained in the Codex with a waypoint to where that questline begins.

Several messages used to mislead. "Update the addon" now names the text that is missing instead of blaming your install, a delve that hides your position says so rather than asking you to try again, and sharing tips to party chat no longer refuses everything because one section of three is empty.
