# Midnight Helper 4.10.1: the real upgrade price

Want new things sooner and help test them? We put out betas regularly: set the release type of Midnight Helper in the
CurseForge app to **Beta**. Found something odd, or does everything just work? Tell us: `/mh report` in game, a comment
here on CurseForge, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.

## My gear: what now?, sharper

- **The real price of every step, discounts included.** An upgrade step costs 20 crests. It costs **half (10)** when
  one of your characters already had a stronger item in that slot and you own the *"… of the Mist"* achievement for
  that kind, and it is **free** when this character already had one. The window now works that out for every item, so
  "You can do 5 steps right now!" means five, and it tells you how many of your steps are free or half price. We
  checked it against the upgrade NPC's own window, step for step.
- **Where every kind of crest comes from.** Under Adventurer, Veteran, Champion and Hero: "Get these crests from: …",
  in the game's own words and your own language.
- It **updates by itself** when you spend crests or upgrade an item.

## Search: mounts from the Mount run

Type a mount from the Mount run, for example *Black Drake* or *Invincible*, and the search bar finds it. A click opens
the Mount run on that raid and points the arrow at the entrance.

New texts in other languages are our own translation: native speakers, corrections are very welcome.
