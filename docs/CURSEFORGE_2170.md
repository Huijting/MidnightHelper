# Midnight Helper 2.17.0 — The Coiled Isle, and what it does not tell you

## /mh curios

Valeera's delve curios are described by name and not much else. This reads every option's real text out of your own game at the moment you ask, so it shows the ranks you actually have rather than a guide's snapshot.

It does not rank them. Which curio is best depends on your spec and the delve you are running, and nobody has measured that — so the command explains and then gets out of the way.

## Treasures that need something done first

Seven Coiled Isle treasures cannot simply be walked to. The route now names the step that unlocks each one and puts its coordinates on a button, instead of sending you to a chest that will not open and letting you work out why.

## The flight map points at the stop

It used to name the stop in a line of text, which left you reading a sentence and then hunting that name across a map full of pins. The pin itself is now highlighted.

## A shortcut to the isle

The portal in Silvermoon City, and the one back, appear in route guidance once you have finished the quest that creates it. Until then they stay hidden. A portal you are told about but cannot use costs more than no portal at all.

## /mh mount

Searches the mount journal the way `/mh ach` searches achievements, and answers from your own client rather than from a guide.

## Fixed

Buttons whose labels ran past the edge of the frame in the longer languages. Criteria that showed a question mark instead of a name, and a crash when a route reached one of them. An error at a delve boss caused by a value the game now keeps secret.
