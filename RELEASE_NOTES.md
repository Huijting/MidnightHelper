# Midnight Helper 4.11.0: the Labyrinth room by room, every play card checked, enchants in colour, and a solo This Week

Want new things sooner and help test them? We put out betas regularly: set the release type of Midnight Helper in the
CurseForge app to **Beta**. Found something odd, or does everything just work? Tell us: `/mh report` in game, a comment
here on CurseForge, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.

## The Labyrinth of Kindo'jan

- **A window per room, by itself.** Walk into a room and a small window opens with what that room's task asks, step
  by step where it helps (Bear's Burden, Containment Breach), plus the tip for the boss of that room. The task names
  are the ones your own game shows.
- **A window per room boss.** `/mh bosscard` lists all of them; click one for only that boss's tips. The search box
  finds them by name too.
- **A book button on the marker bar** inside the Labyrinth: left-click for this room, right-click for the boss list.
- **Kindo'jan**: his window opens by itself when you reach *Enemy of the Labyrinth*. New in his tips:
  - *Claws of the Empire* is just his normal attack. You cannot dodge it and it gives no Disgraceful Display stack.
  - Drop *Erratic Mojo* near the edge, but **not close to Kindo'jan**: his hit can throw you off the platform there.
  - **Stuck with 0 lives** and he keeps coming back? Do not leave the delve: the **coiled rope by the closed door at
    the start** takes you to the treasure room. Its spot is a link in the Codex.
- **Every Bountiful Coffer needs one Restored Coffer Key.** The Heavy Trunks need none.
- **Your Labyrinth runs count for the Gilded Stash** now, old runs included.
- **Loa-Spirit Conduits** (Wind, Earth, Fire, Mist): what each one does for 2 minutes, and that since Blizzard's
  hotfix of 20 October they no longer work in Kindo'jan's fight. Use them on the way.
- **Flooded Chamber:** the chest route leads you to the underwater entrance first. The Fiery Hexmask melts the ice.

## Corrosive Codex

- **Click a situation** on the advice card next to the Codex (*Kindo'jan*, *Groups of enemies take too long*, …) and
  the gifts for it **light up in Blizzard's window**. You click them yourself: a gift you have not unlocked yet costs
  Corrosive Souls, so Midnight Helper never picks for you.
- The card has a **solid background** again and **sees your gifts** again after Blizzard renumbered the buttons.
- **All twelve unlocked?** The Codex card says what your spare souls are still good for.

## Character window: enchants in colour

The gear panel next to your character window now has a line per enchant slot at level 90:
**green** = the enchant we advise, **orange** = another enchant, **red** = no enchant or an empty socket, **yellow** =
we can't tell. Click a line for the advice. The enchant list comes from Blizzard's own game data, both ranks included.
A tie for your best stat (Frost Mage: Mastery and Crit) now counts both.

## Every How you play card checked

All 40 **How you play** cards were checked line by line, by two separate routes: the game data itself and the
current guides. Where the two did not agree, a third check decided. What changed:

- **One line on top of each card** says it follows the talents most players pick. Missing a button? Then it is a
  talent you did not choose. **At the bottom** each card now says on which day it was checked.
- **Where a step needs a talent you may not have**, the step says so: "if you took …".
- **Wrong or misleading lines fixed**, for example Balance's Eclipse button, Blood's Bone Shield, Vengeance's
  defensives, Devourer's hero tree, and on the healer cards Intercession's cost, Abundance, Healing Rain (up to 6
  people) and Master the Darkness. Judgements like "best this season" are gone where the guides disagree.
- **Stay alive:** Purifying Brew when Stagger turns yellow or red, Zephyr is a big defensive, Lichborne is for fear
  and charm, Shield Block and Ignore Pain once the fight begins, Feint against area damage, and their own advice for
  Demon Spikes, Deep Breath and Survival of the Fittest.
- **Healers:** dispels show Poison, Disease or Curse only when you have the talent for it; Discipline gives Power
  Infusion to a damage dealer; Holy Priest's Apotheosis is for using often.
- **The window scrolls** when a card is taller than your screen, the tabs stay on top, and the lines no longer run
  over each other after Shift+scroll.

## Your keys

- **One window for all keybind help:** the first card in the Tools room, or `/mh mykeys`. Three buttons: your key
  block, *Which spells do I press?* and your whole keyboard, now also at level 90.
- **Counting which spells you press is on by default**, so the swap tips just appear. You can still turn it off.
- **Click a spell** in *Which spells do I press?* and the key it sits on lights up on the keyboard.
- **A quieter keyboard screen:** the colour legend and the filters sit behind a *More* button.

## This Week

- **Mostly solo?** A switch at the top of This Week puts delves first: your Restored Coffer Keys and shards,
  Valeera, and *Next up* tells you to use a key on a Bountiful delve. Mythic+, raids, the world boss, group buffs and
  the dungeon weekly are hidden.
- **Shorter for everyone:** the story campaign, rares, mounts and the daily tip fold into *Also handy*, and
  *Later, as you level* is one line on a low character.
- **In the game now:** a news banner on top, and an orange *News* button in the title bar.
- **Vaeli's housing weekly** is on the list.
