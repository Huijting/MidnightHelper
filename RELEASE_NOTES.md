# Midnight Helper 5.0.2: more help in WoW Forever, and nameplate colours explained

Want new things sooner and help test them? We put out betas regularly: set the release type of Midnight Helper in the
CurseForge app to **Beta**. Found something odd, or does everything just work? Tell us: `/mh report` in game, a comment
here on CurseForge, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.

Mostly new things for **WoW Forever**, and a few for both games.

## WoW Forever

- **Train all:** a button under the game's own trainer window. One click learns everything you can afford, and MH tells
  you what still waits for more money.
- **Find your trainer:** *Take me to my trainer* now puts a **cross above your trainer** when you get close. The arrow
  stays until you stand next to them, and both go away when you talk to the trainer. Each trainer has one place, from
  the most reliable source.
- **Missing buffs:** the button you know from Midnight, now in Forever. It shows when you miss a buff you can cast on
  yourself (Power Word: Fortitude, Arcane Intellect, your armor, aura, shield, aspect, weapon buff, or your pet), and a
  click casts it in **your highest rank**. A buff from another player counts too.
- **Bags almost full:** a message in the middle of your screen at 2 free bag slots (and when your bags are full), with
  how many grey items you can sell and for how much. `/mh bags` changes the number or turns it off.
- **Rest first:** when you target an enemy with low mana or health, *Low mana: drink first* or *Low health: eat first*.
  Forever hides your mana and health from addons, so the game itself decides when you see it. `/mh rest off` turns it
  off.
- **Your last tool:** a warning before you sell or destroy your last Mining Pick, Skinning Knife, Blacksmith Hammer or
  fishing pole, with a buy-back button if it already happened. Shamans see which Totem item a totem spell still needs.

## Both games

- **Nameplate colours:** using **MF Nameplates**? `/mh colors` (or the card in the Tools room) explains every colour on
  your nameplates, read from your own MF settings: enemies, casters, aggro for your role, and the cast bar. Without MF
  Nameplates you see a short tip instead.
- **A newer version:** when someone in your guild or group runs a newer Midnight Helper, one line in chat tells you.
  `/mh version off` turns it off.
- **Key block:** before you place it, the window names the game keys it takes over (for example C for your character
  window) and where to give them a new key.

## Fixes

- The command `/mh trainer here` was shown translated, and so did not work, in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and
  Russian.
- The Midnight start page keeps a small line that Midnight Helper also works in WoW Forever.

Have fun!
