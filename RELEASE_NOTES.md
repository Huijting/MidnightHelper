# Midnight Helper 4.15.0: a search bar that finds everything, and settings that show what they do

Want new things sooner and help test them? We put out betas regularly: set the release type of Midnight Helper in the
CurseForge app to **Beta**. Found something odd, or does everything just work? Tell us: `/mh report` in game, a comment
here on CurseForge, or the feedback form on midnighthelper.com.

## A search bar that finds everything

Type what you are looking for in the search bar at the top of Midnight Helper.

- **Every single setting.** Type "arrow size" and you land on that one setting, which blinks so you see it, instead of
  on a page with eighty rows.
- **A typo is fine.** "arow" still finds the arrow.
- **Words that mean the same.** "audio" finds the sound settings, "alert" finds the warnings. This works in every
  language Midnight Helper speaks.
- **Keys too.** Walk the list with the arrow keys or Tab, and press Enter to open.
- **Much more to find:**
  - The places in the Silvermoon city guide: type **ah**, **bank**, **mail** or **inn** and the arrow takes you there.
  - The *How you play* card of every spec: type **frost** and pick Frost Mage or Frost Death Knight.
  - The four world bosses, the Showdowns, the Trading Post tab, every curio and the Mount run window.
  - Treasures and glyphs that have no spot on the map.

## Settings that show what they do

On the *All settings* page:

- **The magnifier** next to a setting shows what it really does, right now: the route arrow, a warning, a sound, a rare
  alert, the notice after a delve. Some settings show it by themselves when you switch them on. Click again to see
  another situation, for example a new record or a slower run.
- **Ctrl+click** puts a setting back to the recommended choice. Every section also has a **Back to recommended**
  button.
- Settings that only work when a switch above them is on are **indented**, and **grey** while that switch is off.

## Key block: new spells

While levelling, your key block can place every new spell for you. The switch for that, **New spells** (ask me / place
automatically / never ask), is now also in *All settings*, so search finds it. `/mh block why` tells you how it is set
and which new spells are waiting for a place.

## Rare route on The Coiled Isle

- The arrow stays with the rare you chose, no longer jumps back when you walk in and out of a door, and names where a
  door leads.
- Szarith the Fanged comes last, with a warning about the elite guards around him.
- *Skip* skips the rare the arrow points at.

## Also

- **Raid shopping list:** "there is a choice" only shows on rows that really have one, also under an item's own
  tooltip.
- **Silvermoon city guide:** the Auction House pin now points at the auctioneers inside.
- **German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian:** the city's own name now replaces the abbreviation "SMC".
- Typing **Sporefall** or **Rotmire** and pressing Enter opens their boss window.

The new texts in German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian and Russian are our own translation: native speakers,
corrections are very welcome.

Have fun!
