MIDNIGHT HELPER - THIRD-PARTY PARTS AND SOURCES
Last checked: 22 November 2026

The MIT licence in LICENSE covers Midnight Helper's own work only. The parts and data of others listed in THIRD_PARTY.txt
keep their own terms.

A = parts and data that come with a licence (full texts in E where they must travel along)
B = shipped parts whose licence is not clear
C = data and facts looked up in sources without a free licence, or with a licence of another kind
D = sources used to read or check facts
E = licence texts

Naming a source below claims no licence, permission or endorsement from it. This list was made from the comments in our
files and from comparing them with the sources we could read; tell us if something is missing:
https://github.com/Huijting/MidnightHelper/issues

    GroupRezLust.lua, SurvivalPlan.lua.

    Modules/ForeverDungeonQuestsData.lua, ForeverClassQuests.lua.

    Modules/ForeverClassQuests.lua.

Polestar, apart from the recordings in A10 (GPL-3.0) (single class quest facts of 17 November 2026)
    Modules/ForeverClassQuests.lua.


====================================================================================================
D. SOURCES USED TO READ OR CHECK FACTS
====================================================================================================

Blizzard Entertainment's game and client data, read in the game and through wago.tools. Wowhead, including its WoW
Forever database. Icy Veins, Method, Archon, Murlok, warcraft.wiki.gg, azeroth.wiki, wowfdb.com. The levelling guides
named per talent order in Modules/ForeverTalentNext.lua. Addons named in our comments as "idea only" or "compared
only" gave no code or text.
